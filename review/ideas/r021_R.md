# R021_R — erase the opposite shore before proving anything about its rows

**Rank 1; lane D.** In marked R(3), `K=2,t=17,m=15`, try **one 29-vertex proof per untouched support**, not one proof per pair of shore profiles. The test needs neither a fixed profile for the retained shore nor an inventory of profiles for the omitted one. It worked on support 404, whose current inventory has 16,060 marked assignments. A second host shows exactly what does *not* transfer. R(7) is already accounted for; this is not a change of status for R(3..6).

All new evidence is in `S=scratch/ideas/r021_R`. The short replay is `bash scratch/ideas/r021_R/replay.sh`. Labels: **hand** for the projection, **cert** for the rebound ordinary CNF, **enum** for the coverage/cost measurements, **estimate** for the extrapolated speed ratio. No full-case claim.

## What survives deletion (hand)

Use D-028 and D-045 (`ralph/lanes/D/TRIPLE18.md`). At a deficient path `a–h–b`, let `A=N(a)−{h}` be the short shore, `B=N(b)−{h}` the long shore, `X=N(h)−{a,b}` the seven poles, and `Y` the 14 untouched sites. Each shore has eight vertices. For `y∈Y` write `S_y=N(y)∩X`; a fixed support gives the actual 14 masks, including multiplicities. In this orientation the exceptional pole 0 has one A contact and all six others have two; every pole has two B contacts. Five A sites have two poles each, three have one each. Write `P_w=N(w)∩X` for `w∈A`.

Keep only `J=G[X∪Y∪A]`; **all eight vertices of B are absent**. Do not pin any `P_w`. The short list below is enough on the pilot host.

1. **Two independence tests, not one:** `α(J)≤8` since `b` misses all of J; `α(J[X∪Y])≤7` since *both* `a,b` miss this smaller board and are nonadjacent. Every nine-subset of J, or eight-subset of `X∪Y`, yields the ordinary clause that at least one internal edge is present. It need not come from the neighborhood of a pole. These clauses remain valid even when every A row is free.
2. Every `w∈A` and every `y∈Y` has 2 or 3 B neighbors. For w this is its codegree with b; for y likewise. The other deficient path is vertex-disjoint from the marked path, so b has no second deficient partner. Similarly y has 2 or 3 A neighbors by its codegree with a. Hence `5−|P_w|≤d_Y(w)≤6−|P_w|` and `6−|S_y|≤d_Y(y)+d_A(y)≤7−|S_y|`. On summing the A degrees, `e(A,Y)=8·8−17−13=34`. Also `e(Y)=15` by definition of the layer. This retains missing-shore information without guessing a single one of its edges.
3. Impose triangle-freeness. Every pair has at most three common neighbors **in J**; reduce that upper bound to two on A/A pairs (they already share a) and X/X pairs (they already share h). X and A separately are independent. Order the five double masks and three singleton masks separately by decreasing binary value. Any physical shore can be sorted this way, including repeated masks; equal-mask sites still have distinct Y edges. Neither the pole labels nor the Y labels are permuted in the proof. The two shores are not exchanged.

These are **necessary**, not sufficient. There is no hidden requirement that the missing B or the second deficient path itself can be reconstructed. Conversely, a contradiction on this weaker board applies simultaneously to every extension of the given Y support. This is why it pays to omit an entire shore rather than merely hide its profile behind a selector.

## A priced proof at one support (measured)

**Target binding.** One-based line **404** of `scratch/D/triple18/roster.jsonl` has triple `7`, ordinary pair word `010111010111100101101`, and marked pole stabilizer one. Use the triple as the first Y site and expand the pair word in lexicographic pole-pair order. The other pole degrees can be recovered from the masks; no D-021-style assumption about locations of the other exceptional endpoints enters the formula.

**Discovery and independent proof.** `shadow.py` adds padded-independent-set clauses from candidate assignments until the projected formula refutes. All such clauses are valid for every graph regardless of whether that candidate or its predecessor was valid. For 404 this took 21.29 CPU-s (257,188 conflicts); 7,526 distinct physical witness sets suffice. `rebind.py` starts over with edge variables for **all** physical pairs, fresh auxiliary-variable numbering, explicit pole and shore units, and full edge disjunctions on the witness sets. The only information it takes from the discovery is the sets of *vertices*: it checks every set's size and location. It does **not** import discovery clauses, an active-rows table, or a list of mate pairs. The fresh 68,864-clause CNF took 0.46 CPU-s to build, 16.10 to generate the LRAT, and 2.22 + 4.53 for `lrat-check` and `cake_lpr`. Total **44.61 CPU-s** across these stages. The 100,177,415-byte raw LRAT is about 29 MiB gzip-1. Evidence: `S/{whole.log,whole-rebound.log,PROOF.sha256,final-replay.log}`.

**Denominator.** D's round-21 `scratch/D/wing21/counts.tsv` is the baseline used here, not the older 1,334,977. `audit.py` independently generates the 4,062 short and 6,202 long profile words. It tests each side by a literal independent-nine search in the graph with every still-possible edge present: **540 short and 661 long** pass at 404. For each pole pair require its total row multiplicity across Y, short and long to be 1 or 2; the poles already share h, which cannot also witness the other deficiency. This gives **16,060**, on **478** distinct active short words. With trivial marked stabilizer these are also 16,060 marked orbits, **6.03% of the 266,500** in D's provisional round-21 roster. These counts price the proof; its validity does not rely on them. They are not 16,060 unmarked graphs or disjoint cases of the Ramsey problem.

## Calibration, limits and handoff

- **Smaller control:** all ten 6-regular graphs in the 191-class order-22 catalogue were screened; three have cap three and supply 125 physical deficient paths, both orientations. Both formulations retained **250/250** planted directed shores using their *actual smaller* degrees and totals. The independent binder imposed **all** appropriately padded five- and six-subset clauses, not just learned ones. Changing one pinned Y-edge while retaining the original edge total failed on **250/250**. `S/{order-cal.log,rebound-cal.log}`.
- **Why both parts matter:** sorted masks remove permutations within each rank without asserting an automorphism. Three unrestricted, unsorted host probes hit 42,000 conflicts; these are not exclusions. Retaining only five physical A vertices instead of all eight admitted relaxed models on the sampled prefixes (`S/{free0,delete5}.log`). D-049's one-shore test uses only forced nonedges; this test couples actual A–Y and Y–Y edges and the exact 15-edge budget. Unlike R015_R/D-044, it never fixes a pair of wing profiles. No claim about C's cap-five card bins.
- **Transfer test, deliberately negative:** 404's 7,526 witness sets are valid on *any* host in the same coordinate convention, but need not suffice. Applied unchanged to **896** (opposite triple orientation, largest surviving trivial-stabilizer host in that class), they gave a satisfying assignment to the weakened board after **552 conflicts**. This is not a realization of that host. Do not re-check the unchanged deck; discover additional witnesses there. `S/transfer.log`.
- **Lane D, first three steps:** (1) Run `S/replay.sh`; bind 404 against the current roster and debit it only once, if accepted. (2) At 896, start from the transferred clauses, separate new physical independent sets on the *unpinned* short shore, and rebuild a fresh proof. Predeclare ≤120 discovery CPU-s and ≤128 MiB compressed proof. (3) If that caps, price just its largest two-double prefix against equally spaced direct proofs; split no other host until the comparison is in. Stop on any lost plant, a genuine model satisfying the full projected tests, or a second priced host with <10× gain over direct proofs. A conflict cap alone does not refute anything.
