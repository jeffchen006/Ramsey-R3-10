## 1. Find critical e108 residues by repairing independent sets exposed by a single vertex

**Mechanism (hand).** Let H be triangle-free with α(H)≤a, and deletion-critical: deleting *any* edge permits an independent (a+1)-set. Choose any vertex v, let S=N_H(v), d=|S|, and thin H−v by legal edge deletions to any edge-minimal spanning graph J with α(J)≤a. Write F=E(H−v)−E(J). Then:

1. No edge of F touches S. For an edge sx of H−v, s∈S, an independent (a+1)-set witnessing criticality must contain s and x, so cannot contain v. It already witnesses criticality in H−v and survives further edge deletions other than sx.
2. Thus S is independent in J, and F consists entirely of pairs on V(J)−S. Each is a nonedge of J with no common neighbour in J. Its cardinality is fixed: `q=e(H)−e(J)−d`.
3. List every independent a-set I of J disjoint from S. Give I a row consisting of its pairs eligible for F. The chosen q pairs must hit every row; otherwise I+v is independent. Conversely, after adding the star vS and q triangle-compatible pairs, this hitting condition suffices for α≤a: a new independent (a+1)-set must use v. Moreover the hit must be **inclusion-minimal**: a witness for a repair edge cannot avoid v, since J still has α≤a after that edge is removed. Finally, for every s∈S some independent a-set of J must intersect S in exactly {s}; remove v from the witness for vs.

`stitch.cc` enumerates S, branches on the uncovered row with fewest eligible pairs, and tests triangle compatibility, private rows for chosen repairs, and the exact repair count. It checks criticality of **every** final edge; minimality of F alone is not enough. The branching partitions solutions by the first selected pair in a row. These statements give exact coverage for a supplied (J,d), not a census of all J. This is not B's one-star rewire, adjacent stem or round-9 nonadjacent pair repartition: edges in the old card change, but *only* to repair independent sets disjoint from the new star. Nor is an order-33 host needed.

## 2. Impose B's outside-prefix e88 anchor on the *parent* before choosing any repair

**Mechanism (hand).** Now H has 30 vertices, 88 edges and α≤8. In the deletion-critical outside-prefix complement, B's `ralph/lanes/B/ANCHOR88.md` guarantees at least four good roots: d=6, b(v)=52 or d=7, b(v)=44, all neighbours of degree ≥5. This uses the prefix **plus** the separately completed d7/e43 case. For a good v form J as in idea 1. Every s∈S has final degree `d_J(s)+1` independent of F. Consequently one can price a whole star before any repair:

- Every s has d_J(s)≥4. Require `Σ_{s∈S}d_J(s)=30` at d=6, `=37` at d=7, since `b(v)=88−Σ_{s∈S}d_H(s)`.
- For degrees 3..8 respectively, B's outside-prefix/GR upper bounds on neighbour-degree sums are `U=(15,23,29,36,44,53)`. At a marked neighbour s, start its sum at `d + Σ_{x∈N_J(s)}d_J(x)`. Each repair endpoint in N_J(s) increments it once. The degree of s cannot change, so crossing `U[d_J(s)+1]` anywhere in the search rejects the entire subtree. Do **not** do this at an arbitrary unmarked vertex: its degree could increase. Check the full profile at leaves.
