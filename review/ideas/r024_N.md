## The reduction (hand)

In a triangle-free graph with independence cap `a`, let `v` have degree `d` and be at distance at most two from all vertices. Choose a **nonempty** subset `U⊆N(v)`, retain `T=N(v)−U`, and put `Y=H−⋃_{u∈U}N[u]`, `t=|T|`, `r=|U|`. Then

```
α(Y) ≤ a−r,       |Y| ≤ t + floor(t ((a−r)+(a−d+1))/2).
```

Indeed `T` survives. Every vertex of `Y−T` misses `U` but sees `T` by domination at `v`. The vertices whose only contact is a particular `b∈T` are independent; appending `N(v)−{b}` shows there are at most `a−d+1` of them. Hence there are at least `2(|Y|−t)−t(a−d+1)` incidences into `T`. Each member of `T` has at most `a−r` neighbours in `Y`: its neighbours are independent and can be padded by `U`. This proves the bound.

For `a=8,d=6`, choosing 3/4/5 neighbours gives caps **15/9/4**. At order 15, the three retained neighbours each have degree five inside `Y`. The other 12 sites have nine singleton contacts (three at each neighbour) and three double contacts. No double pair repeats: two copies, three singletons at one endpoint and the other four original anchors would be an independent nine. This is a *forced* marked `(3,6;15)` card, not a generic `(3,6;17)` upper bound. The same 15-card lemma applies at a degree-seven root of the order-40 MTF problem (`a=9,d=7,r=4`); no gain is claimed there.

## From the low graph to a physical proof

Assume for contradiction that all degree-six punctures have at least 69 edges. Use B-040 and B's confirmed round-23 pair cut (`ralph/critic/round_023.md`, section B): degrees are 6–8, each low neighbour-degree sum is 38 or 39, and every low/low pair has codegree at most two. Thus `s=8+n8`, and `L` is triangle-free of degrees 3–5. For `U⊆N_L(v)`, a lower bound needing **no high edges or high labels** is

```
|Y| ≥ 32 − |⋃_{u∈U} N_L[u]| − Σ_{u∈U}(6−d_L(u)).
```

The last sum counts high neighbours with multiplicity; overlaps only strengthen the lower bound. `low.cc` tests it against 15/9/4. Counts are of unlabelled *low graphs*, not residues:

| `s` | triangle-free, degrees 3–5 | low/low codegree ≤2 | pass window bound |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 | 8 | 2 | 1 |
| 9 | 22 | 1 | 0 |
| 10 | 190 | 13 | 5 |

`low{8,9,10}.log`: `geng -tqd3D5 n` then `low`. `audit_layers.py` independently extends all eligible seven-vertex Atlas graphs by independent rows and compares isomorphism classes, not hashes. In the cube a triple gives a lower bound of 16; on the unique nine-site diagram four neighbours give 11 against 9 (`audit_layers.log` gives actual vertices). The sole eight-site survivor is the Wagner graph `L=C8(1,4)`.

**Wagner.** Each low site `v` has three high neighbours `T_v`. All highs have degree seven. In the window for `U=N_L(v)` at most nine of the 24 highs are removed, so equality at 15 is forced. Those three low neighbours therefore have disjoint high-neighbour sets. Any two nonadjacent lows have a common low neighbour in the Wagner graph; adjacent lows cannot share a high either. Hence all 24 highs have exactly one owner, in eight independent triples `T_i`.

For `y∈T_i`, five high neighbours lie in its 15-window, and exactly one high neighbour lies in a group at a neighbour of `i` in `L`. The five meet all four groups at nonneighbours of `i` (saturation for `y,j`); their counts are `2,1,1,1`. The edges between groups at adjacent lows consequently form a matching of size 12. Each of the 12 low edges `ij` must receive such an edge: otherwise `T_i∪T_j` is independent and can be joined to an independent three in `L−{i,j}`. For example the eight open neighbourhoods are independent triples and at most six meet `i` or `j`. Thus **one** edge per low edge. Label the three highs in each group by the low neighbour at the other end of that matching edge; this uses no automorphism of `H`.

Now retain only groups `0,2,3,5,6` (the 15-window at 0). Fixed matching edges on `2–3,2–6,5–6` leave **63** unknown edges. Require triangle-freeness, no independent six, at least one and at most two contacts per far group, and at most one excess contact above these minima per high vertex across the retained far groups. `wagner5.cnf` has 6,822 clauses and a 448,761-byte LRAT accepted by both checkers. Changing the cap to six gives SAT: the order-15 independence constraint matters. A separate catalogue calculation (`wagner.cc`, `wagner.log`) finds 499 marked stars in all 64,732 order-15 graphs; none of 178,990 unlabelled-complement partitions into four independent triples passes even the weaker near/far degree test (each partition tries role permutations). The certificate route needs none of those 64,732 source graphs.

## Shared edges pay again at ten lows (measured enum + local cert)

All five surviving ten-site diagrams are cubic. Every low has three d7 contacts and neither d8 vertex touches a low. In shapes **0 and 1** of `low10.rem` (literal graph6 `I?Bcu\x60gM?`, with `\x60` a single backtick, and `I?bFB_wF?`), an allowed pair of low contacts is nonadjacent with internal codegree zero or one. The 20 such pairs contain no allowed triple. All ten codegree-zero pairs must occur by saturation; each low belongs to two of these, leaving one free incidence. Any additional pairs form a matching in the 20-pair graph; unmatched sites receive one singleton. Empty high masks fill the degree inventory. This enumerates all possible low-to-high incidences up to relabelling identical highs, **without** assuming the high graph is maximal on its own.

Each shape starts with 763 matchings; the 15-site bound with exact high unions leaves **123**. A direct matching enumerator and a Boolean population census agree on the literal sets, and the `Aut(L)` orbit expansion is identical, including stabilizers: **14 representatives per shape** (`matchings.log`, `fiber{0,1}.roster`).

At each tight window the low/low and low/high adjacencies are known, and intersecting high supports forbid their edge. Impose triangle-freeness, independence cap five, and the equality degree/contact rows above. An unknown edge is keyed by its **two original physical endpoints**. Use that same bit wherever another window sees the edge; do *not* identify edges merely because their masks agree. Any real completion satisfies the union. `fiber.py` emits the union up to the first separately contradictory window, or all tight windows otherwise.
