**Notation.** In R(3), mark a nonedge `ab` of codegree one with unique path `a-h-b`. Write `A=N(a)−h`, `B=N(b)−h` (eight each), `X=N(h)−{a,b}` (seven), `Y` for the 14 sites avoiding all three closed neighborhoods, and `Q=X∪Y`. The sets are disjoint by triangle-freeness and the unique witness. Let `t=e(A,B)` count pentagons through the path, `m=e(Y)`, `K` the total number of deficient pairs. The 19-site book has `18+t` edges, and 9-regularity implies `2(e(Q)−e(book))=9(21−19)`. Hence `e(Q)=27+t`. Since `X` is independent,

`e(X,Y) = 27+t−m`. Each `x∈X` has `l_x=|N(x)∩A|≤2` and `r_x=|N(x)∩B|≤2` (its codegrees with `a,b` include `h`). Thus `d_Y(x)=8−l_x−r_x≥4`, and

`m = t−1 − Σ_{x∈X}(4−l_x−r_x)`.

## 1. Two multigraphs on seven sites fix the two shores (hand + measured enum; two local certs)

**Block.** `K=2, t=17, m=16` at the chosen path. An exhaustive negative would forbid a 17-pentagon path with every middle neighbor hitting both wings twice. It would not forbid `t=17,m≤15`, `t>17`, or any other value of `K`.

**Why the support is small.** D-028 gives two vertex-disjoint deficient paths, including their witnesses. At `K=2` these are the only two. In particular **h cannot be incident to any deficient pair**, nor can it witness the other deficiency. These are different facts; both are used. Equality `m=t−1` forces `d_Y(x)=4` and `l_x=r_x=2` at all seven `x`. For `y∈Y`, `c(h,y)=|N(y)∩X|≥2`. These 14 integers sum to 28, so each is exactly two. Consequently the 14 physical Y-sites are the 14 edges, counted with multiplicity, of a loopless four-regular multigraph `L` on X. Each pair of X-sites already shares h, so multiplicity is at most two. This derivation needs neither a guessed 17-edge wing shape nor any enumeration of the edges on Y.

**Reject a support before placing any Y-edge.** For any independent `I⊆Y`, `|I|≤|N_X(I)|`: otherwise `a,b`, `I`, and `X−N(I)` form an independent ten or larger. Two Y-sites whose supports intersect cannot be adjacent. Thus any pairwise-intersecting edge multiset of L must have at least as many incident X-sites as edges. For example, four pairwise-intersecting edges on three poles are fatal. The generator finds all minimal deficient subsets and asks for at least one Y-edge internal to each; empty clauses give literal witnesses. An independent audit searches the pairwise-intersecting subsets directly. **33 → 6** supports; 27 fail without a variable Y-edge. All six admit a local 16-edge Y-filling in the Hall/degree/triangle relaxation. Controlling for symmetry: 52,065 different four-regular multiplicity-≤2 words on seven *labelled* poles, equal to the stabilizer-weighted sum over the 33. `S/{audit-support.log,pure.txt,p17.jsonl}`. Do not purchase an LP or SAT call for each Y-filling.

**Instead complete both wings' supports, leaving all 16 Y-edges variable.** Each `w∈A` has `1≤|N_X(w)|≤2`: its codegree with `h` is `1+|N_X(w)|`. On this shore the sum is 14. Exactly six sites have two poles and two have one. Hence a shore support is a second loopless multigraph `P` with **six** edges on X, degree ≤2; put `2−d_P(x)` singleton sites at each x. Use `P'` for B. If `l,p,p'` are the three multiplicities at a pole pair, then

`1 ≤ l+p+p' ≤ 2`.

Indeed their common neighbors are exactly `h` and the exterior sites containing both poles. Codegree one here would give a second deficient path with witness h. Conversely this interval is only a necessary row; passing it is not a completion.

## 2. Sparse untouched graphs force deficient partners at the middle (hand + measured enum)

**Block and scoped result for audit.** In *any* R(3) deficient path, `m≥10`. At `m=10`, at least **two distinct y∈Y** have `c(h,y)=1`; equivalently `m≥11` when h has at most one such partner. This does **not** assume `t=16` or `K=2`. The only external structural obstruction used here beyond cap/regularity is the confirmed no-`K3,3` lemma (`ralph/lanes/D/SATURATED_BICLIQUE.md`).

For an independent `I⊆Y`, adjoining `a,b` shows `α(Y)≤7`. For each `x∈X` its row `M=N_Y(x)` is independent, `|M|≥4` by the displayed degree identity, and `α(Y−M)≤6` by padding with `a,b,x`. Also `|M∩N_Y(y)|≤3` for every y. Consider **seven rows chosen from this finite list**, relaxed to nonnegative multiplicities totalling seven. Each point y occurs 1..3 times (`c(h,y)`), each pair at most twice (three X-rows through a pair would give `K3,3` with h), each triple at most once (two X-sites cannot have h plus three Y-sites in common). For the second assertion give a total debit of one against the lower bound of two occurrences per point. These are all necessary restrictions on the *same seven rows*.

Choosing one endpoint of each edge gives a vertex cover, so `α(Y)≥14−m` and we can start at `m=7`. At `m=7,8,9,10` there are respectively just **1,1,5,17** triangle-free Y types with `α≤7`. Two independent coverage routes agree: direct `geng -t` vs. multisets of connected triangle-free components (no connected component can exceed order 11 at ten edges), with literal independence rechecked on the survivors. At `m≤9`, all seven types have no rows or an exact integer separating inequality. At ten, 15/17 fail; the remaining `P4+P6+2K2` and `P8+3K2` both fail with the single debit. The audit uses explicit vertex sets and maximal independent sets, and rebuilds each nonzero dual coefficient, without the LP generator. `S/{unlow.jsonl,limited.jsonl,audit_sparse.py,audit-sparse.log}`. Controls: all 125 smaller physical row populations satisfy their own debit budgets and 4,651 tested inequalities (`S/sparsecal.log`).

**Gain and limitation.** The result can be checked on **24 small graphs**, independent of how many full wings or second-deficiency roles exist. It buys a global row `Σ(4−l_x−r_x)≤t−11`, one tighter when at most one Y-debit is available. **Do not run another scalar LP wave:** the old 77 surviving `t=13` wings remain 77/77 fractionally feasible even with the stronger row (`S/wingcut13.log`). The gain requires the identity or the shape of Y, not just its mean edge count. At `t=13,m=12` (maximal) there are 369 order-14/12 Y types with alpha ≤7 and the seven-X LP cuts 143; **226 fractional**, no 226 negative certificates (`S/t13m12.log`). Thus the small graph gives an inexpensive exact split, not an argument to scan millions of Y-fillings. New versus D's contact occupancies: the induced graph *away* from all three path sites is fixed first; its edges impose named triple and identical-row overlap bounds before any wing graph is chosen. No projected speed-up for undivided R(3) is supported.

**D/H: first three steps.** (1) Audit the two tiny rosters and their sparse integer duals; kill on a missing component or planted row. (2) Put the one-debit implication in a child search that actually fixes Y or the exceptional partners of h, not in the already negative wing LP. Test it on the two named ten-edge graphs before attaching either wing. (3) If pursuing `t=13`, compare *integral* seven-row packages on ≤20 of the 226 upper-end survivors against the same roots without the row hit constraints. Stop if it removes no whole host or leaves >1,000 packages per host. This is lower priority than the priced 17-pentagon slice.
