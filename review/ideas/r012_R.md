# r012_R — determine the same nine cross-edges from two smaller Ramsey graphs

**Rank 1 (only recommendation), lane C.** In the open regular cap-five block, start with a marked `K₄,₄` and two vertices missing both sides. Removing one root from each defining independent four-set gives two ways of reading the same 3×3 matrix of edges. Reject row choices unless one reading is the transpose of the other. The useful object is a table of *actual neighbourhoods*, not another histogram or a new SAT encoding. No assumption that an R(5) graph must contain this marking; no change of case status.

### Why two readings exist (`hand`)

Let the sides be A and B. Write F_A for the induced graph on the vertices outside A and its open neighbourhood, and similarly F_B. Both have independence number at most five. Outside the eight roots no vertex sees both sides (triangle). Hence the windows cover 32 vertices; their overlap Z consists precisely of the vertices seeing neither side. Each window has order at most 17 by `notes/DEPENDENCIES.md` (D-R1). At |Z|=2 both have order 17 and each has 15 private vertices. The seven order-17 source classes are in `gluing/val/r3_6_17.g6` (D-G3). The other possibilities, up to side exchange, are 16/17 at |Z|=1 and 15/17 or 16/16 at |Z|=0. They are **not** in the pilot.

In F_B the four neighbourhoods of the A roots outside B are independent five-sets. Call them marks. They cover precisely the 15 private sites. Each pair of marks overlaps in at most one site: their centres already share all four B vertices. A site in exactly one mark is a *singleton*. Every frame has at least two marks with exactly **three singletons**:

- Total excess multiplicity is 20−15=5. A site of multiplicity r consumes r−1 excess but consumes `binom(r,2)` of the six pair slots. There is at most one triple site, and no quadruple.
- Without a triple there are five doubles, the edges of K₄ minus one edge on the four labels. Singleton counts are 3,3,2,2. With a triple, the three doubles use the three pairs joining its labels to the fourth label. Counts are 3,3,3,2. The same holds on the opposite side.

Take a root a in A with singleton group S of size three in F_B. **Undo just a in the definition of F_A:** the antineighbourhood of A−{a} is exactly `F_A ∪ {a} ∪ S`. Indeed the newly admitted sites are a itself and sites which saw a but none of the other three. No B vertex is admitted. This order-21 graph has α≤6. The degree of a in it is three, and deleting its closed neighbourhood recovers the *same labelled* F_A. Thus the three neighbourhoods `N(x)∩F_A`, x∈S, must be an entry of a short table for that physical F_A. Permute the three rows; do not permute away the two labelled vertices of Z. Symmetrically each three-singleton group T on the B side has a table of neighbourhoods in F_B.

For x∈S a row M must satisfy `M∩Z=N_F_B(x)∩Z` and `|M|=8−d_F_B(x)+|M∩Z|`: x spends one edge on its A root, and the two windows count Z twice. For y∈T the row M′ recovered in the opposite direction must satisfy **`y∈M ⇔ x∈M′`**. This fixes all nine entries of S×T twice. Apply this condition to *every* pair of three-singleton groups, iteratively deleting packages with no mate. The test is necessary, not sufficient: different rectangles can prefer incompatible packages.

### A catalogue-free table, independently checked (`enum`)

On each physical 17-host H enumerate unordered multisets of three independent sets `M₁,M₂,M₃`. The root and its three neighbours give the stated order-21 extension iff every subset J of the three spokes satisfies `α(H − ⋃_{j∈J} M_j) ≤ 6−|J|`. Proof: an independent set using the root can add only vertices of H, at most five; an independent set not using it selects some spokes and then vertices avoiding exactly their neighbourhoods. Triangle-freeness holds because each row is independent and the spokes have no mutual edges. Do **not** require the three masks to be distinct. `direct.cpp` tabulates α on all 2¹⁷ subsets once per H. The direct generator took **0.03 user CPU-s** and emitted 9,959 aligned multisets.

Independent source: extract all 5,702 physical degree-three roots from the 1,118,436 order-21 classes, identify the residual 17-host and expand by its automorphisms. This gives **byte-identical** row tables, with 1,499 / 1,574 / 2,214 / 1,290 / 2,887 / 223 / 272 entries on the seven hosts. No order-20 catalogue, labelled 40-vertex SAT run or assumption about the symmetry of G is needed.

### How much work it removes (`enum` on the indicated inputs)

`S/` means `scratch/ideas/r012_R/`. A frame is one physical catalogue host plus four unordered marks, not a 40-vertex isomorphism class. Two enumerators (marks first in Python; choose the two omitted vertices first in C++) agree on every one of **55,055 frames**, of which 200 have a triple site. They check degrees and codegrees already spent and agree on 3,194 smaller frames at order ten (`framecal2.log`, `frameaudit.log`). We use ordered pairs of frames (reuse allowed), with both possible bijections on Z. Before using the tables, align the Z edge and the complementary Z degrees, cap the known common neighbours of the two Z sites, and equate the total numbers of missing cross edges on the two sides. This leaves exactly **157,472,001** of 6,062,106,050 marked alignments (`S/headers.py`, `S/allheaders.log`).

### Controls, novelty and first three steps

- **Cheapest calibration:** order-11 degree-two lift onto the three order-8 (3,4) hosts: 102 physical roots, exact equality of 366 aligned entries with the independent direct generator. On all ten 6-regular (3,7;22) controls, both frame extraction and terminal propagation retained all 2,118 marked oriented-side squares; all 4,236 planted lifted row multisets were recovered. On their 3×3 rectangles the actual C++ transpose routine accepted 3,448 planted matrices and rejected all 31,032 single-entry corruptions. These are positive and adverse controls, not an order-40 proof.
- **What is new:** `r009_R` and C-031/C-033 match complementary degrees on *shared* sites; here that test alone still leaves 157M alignments. The new data are crossing **edges** between *private* sites, obtained by lifting each window separately. The same 3×3 block is read twice, with no SAT decision on any of its entries. D-004's K₄,₄ obstruction is for cap four, not this cap-five block. This is not C's current same-five reroot of an order-22 mark: the construction uses only seven order-17 bases in production.
- **Replay:** `bash scratch/ideas/r012_R/replay.sh`. Fixed source digests and generated outputs: `S/sources.sha256`, `S/results.sha256`. See `S/replay.log` for the bounded one-core replay. No detached work or change to the shared ledger.
