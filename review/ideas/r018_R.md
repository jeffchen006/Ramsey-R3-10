# R018_R — Re-root a triple site as a fifth pole before joining the frames

**Rank 1, lane C.** In cap-five Case R, a site in three marks of a `K4,4` frame gives a `K3,5`, with that site as the fifth pole. The five-pole view can contradict **both frames together without choosing any crossing edge**. The first cut is small on C's full frontier but exact on its marked slice. The next experiment is to price the same filter one vertex below the extremal core, not to enumerate 2.54 billion headers. R(7) is already closed; this is not a change of status for R(3..6).

Replay from the repository root: `bash scratch/ideas/r018_R/replay_projection.sh`. It regenerates the marked weights and checks C's terminal-free subtotal against the frozen output (`S/clean.log`).

### The cut (hand, with a small published catalogue dependency)

Let `A,B` be the sides of the new biclique, of sizes three and five. Let `H` be the induced graph on sites outside the biclique that miss B, and let `Q` be the remaining exterior sites. No exterior site can see both shores. The three A-neighborhoods outside B are disjoint independent fours in H: each pair of A vertices already shares all five B poles. Also `α(H)≤4` since B can be adjoined. Consequently `12≤|H|≤13`; at 13 it is `C13(1,5)`. The opposite window avoiding A always has order 20. At `|H|=13` it consists of Q plus the one H vertex missed by the three fours.

Record the nonempty B-support of every physical Q site, retaining repetitions. Every pole occurs six times. At `|H|=13` there are 19 sites and 30 incidences. Each pole b has at most **two** singleton sites (support `{b}`). Indeed the closed antineighborhood of the other four poles consists exactly of H, b and these singleton sites. It is triangle-free with independence number at most five. With three singleton sites its order is 17 and b has degree three, whereas all seven order-17 catalogue graphs have minimum degree at least four; four or more would contradict `R(3,6)=18`. See `notes/DEPENDENCIES.md` D-G3/D-G3b and `gluing/val/r3_6_17.g6`. This is a restored **degree** restriction, stronger than the order bound alone.

Let `s` count all singleton sites and let `E` sum `max(0,|support|−2)` over Q. Counting incidences gives **`s=8+E≤10`**. In particular one site on four poles consumes the entire excess budget: all five poles have exactly two singletons, the other eight sites are doubles, and there cannot be another site on three or more poles.

To use this in C's marked 16/17 roster, take a triple site x in the 16-window W. Its three original root neighbors become A; the four opposite roots together with x become B. Now `H=W−N_W[x]`. The fourth original root on A's side is a Q site on exactly four poles. If `d_W(x)=2`, then `|H|=13`, so a triple site **in the opposite frame** is impossible: it would see at least three of the four original B poles, independent of its unknown adjacency to x. No polarity choice or assumption about that crossing is necessary. In particular both frames can pass every old *individual* group test and still have this obstruction.

**Next experiment: `|H|=12`, still without a header product.** For any triple-site frame fix H physically. On the opposite frame enumerate the possible independent neighborhood of x, respecting its remaining degree and its fixed adjacency to the old neutral. This supplies the fifth bit on every old opposite private site. The remaining Q sites and bits are determined on the old window (including x's old-window neighbors outside the root shores and the fourth root). For each pole with k singleton sites, restoring it requires a graph `H+b+S_b` of order `13+k`, with `degree(b)=k`, whose closed-neighborhood deletion gives this **same H**. Thus `k=4` uses only the degree-four roots of the seven order-17 hosts (21 physical roots total); `k=3` uses the 1,458 degree-three roots among the 2,576 order-16 hosts. Carry isomorphisms of the residual H and the actual singleton labels; known H edges, neutral membership and contacts already fixed in either frame must agree. A header needs one fifth-bit row supporting *all* restorations, not an independent choice of x for each pole. Omission of any valid row would invalidate a cut. This tests the multiple-mark site that the original singleton-group transposes did not restore. Cache by physical H, the known labels and the four old support classes; if a key loses binding information, keep all its possibilities. The 21 rooted cards versus `C(2^12,4)` nominal row choices motivates tabulation, **not** an end-to-end speed estimate.

**Calibration and first three steps.** (1) Independently derive the excess identity and project the two quadruple words; reproduce the three table rows and recover the 56 physical bindings before crediting any terminal savings. (2) In the unique regular order-35 control use `K2,4`: all **105** codegree-four pairs have the same 13-core and two singletons per pole; the analogue has 16 Q sites and four poles, so do not apply the target's `s=8+E` there. `S/control.py`, `control.log`, `smalljoin.log`: all 420 marked two-pole restorations and 210 disjoint pairs of restored cards survived physical rebinding. For the proposed 12-core extension, separately check recovery of every rooted 16/17 catalogue card from its deletion and a deliberately corrupted binding before pricing. (3) Test the new fifth-bit rule on a held-out old-marginal-positive 12-core bin. Compare with the unchanged terminal on identical labelled headers. Stop unless no plant is lost and either the bin dies wholesale or the measured number of terminal calls falls at least 100-fold **including** the cost of listing the fifth-bit rows. Do not infer that reduction from the 13-core pilot.

### Five-line summary
- C: treat a triple site as a fifth pole. When the five poles leave a 13-vertex core, test both frames before choosing any crossing edge.
- With a four-pole site, the incidence budget leaves no room for an opposite triple site.
- This rejects 25,987 raw 16/17 headers; exactly 56 passed both old marginal tests, all at `(66,4)`.
- The order-35 control retained all 420 marked pair cards. Six unpinned target SAT pilots hit their conflict caps.
- Audit the 56 bindings, then price the 12-core extension on one held-out bin. R(5) remains open; see `review/ideas/r018_R.md:1`.
