## The reduction (hand)

Use G-043 and `ralph/lanes/G/rows/e170_equality_signatures.md`. At adjacent degree-six roots `u,v`, the wings `L,R` each have five anchors; the only crossing edge is `L₀R₀`. Each of the 28 common exterior sites has a singleton or pair support on **each** wing. There are four private sites per anchor and eight distinct double supports per shore. Call the two missing pairs on a shore its *holes*. Let `h` count sites double on both sides. Then `12+h` sites are private on both sides. Put their multiplicities in a 5×5 matrix `M`; row and column are the two exact supports. At `h=8`, `M` is the off-diagonal grid up to relabelling (r015_N).

1. Retain every site of `M`, including separate vertices for repeated entries, and **one shared set of grid-edge variables**. Fix the eight left double supports. Make one slot per left double and `4−row_sum(i)` singleton slots at `i`. Make right slots analogously, but **offer all ten right pairs**. For each left/right slot combination introduce a selector for that possible site. Omit singleton/singleton combinations (already in `M`) and pairs containing both crossed anchors. Select exactly one entry per left slot and per right singleton slot, at most one per right double. Repeated singleton slots can be labelled arbitrarily. The narrower variant fixes both hole lists and uses every slot.
2. Each empty cell `(i,j) ≠ (0,0)` needs a selected common neighbour of `Lᵢ,Rⱼ` by MTF; no root, wing anchor or private/private site can supply it. This is a **disjunction of selectors**, not a loop over witnesses.
3. For a possible site `z` retain only its edges to grid sites disjoint from its support. Forbid triangles on the grid and in the grid together with `z`. **No variable relates two possible sites.** For `T` of size at most six consisting of grid sites and at most one `z`, let `A,B` be the unused wing coordinates. The maximum independent padding from the wings and roots is `max(|A|+|B|−[0∈A∩B], 1+|A|, 1+|B|)`. If `|T|+pad ≥ 10`, require an edge inside `T`, guarded by `¬select(z)` if present. Pairs sharing an anchor cannot supply an edge.

**Why sound.** A graph supplies one grid graph, an injection of its sites into the selector array and the grid-neighbours of each selected site. Set all edges incident to unused options false; this satisfies even the unguarded triangle clauses. A violated guarded row exhibits ten independent vertices. The converse is not asserted: the relaxation ignores all edges between selected sites, all degree/edge budgets and all blockers needing two possible sites. In particular it does **not** declare unmodelled pairs nonadjacent.

**What is new.** r015_N/G rounds 16–17 fixed the bijection before each terminal SAT call; transferable proofs still specified 4–7 paired doubles. One or two fixed doubles tested alone admitted models. Here **all alternatives must use the same grid-edge assignment** and obey the slot counts and missing-cell demands. No two optional vertices appear in one padded set. Unlike the old free-population MIP, there are no integer population or edge tables. On `leaf+matching.0` the grid alone still has a model even with private matching rows; the successful selector proof uses **no** such rows or degree counters (`scout.log`, `ablation.log`).

## One defect: six private grids instead of a nine-slot permutation search

**Grid bound (hand).** Let row `i` have sum `s` and `t` distinct noncross columns occupied. Of the four private sites of `Lᵢ`, `4−s` have right double support. The matching to the four noncross right anchors has size at least `3+[i=0]` (G-043). Private/private sites match at most `t` anchors, and each other site contributes at most **one** matching edge, even if it meets two anchors. Thus `s−t ≤ 1−[i=0]`. Transpose for columns. Include `M₀₀=0` and margins at most four. A doubled noncross cell uses the entire collision allowance of its row and column. These are necessary conditions, not realizability tests.

At `h=7` exactly one row and one column have sum three; the others sum to four. `defect.py` lists the boards. Independent Burnside counting on `S₄ × S₄` with transposition (`burnside.cc`) gives **1,296 labelled boards, six orbits**. With two hole lists this gives **1,213** contexts, also obtained by explicit stabilizer expansion. Orient each board representative once and fix only its left holes: **8+18+8+11+18+18 = 81** contexts suffice. The 107 in `burnside.tsv` instead distinguish the choice of shore *before* identifying transposed boards. On each side one deficient singleton slot joins the eight double slots.

## Calibration, stopping rule and handoff

- **Calibration (enum).** Use wings of size three, adjacent degree-four roots, one crossing and two private sites per anchor. For exact small enumeration include all edges/triangles and blockers, and do not require MTF. At orders 15 and 16 the aligned canonical class sets agree with the known **24/0**. With one private defect, independently scan all **64,732/2,576** graphs without assuming a grid: **174/11** marked root pairs in **129/11** classes. Both fixed-right and any-right generators recover exactly the same canonical sets (`extract.cc`, `final.control.log`). The binder rejects a spurious grid-edge unit. A small-control mismatch kills the encoding.
- **Negative boundary.** Dropping both fixed hole lists and demanding eight unspecified distinct pairs per shore reached a 15 s solver limit in three variants. Replacing the eight-pair demand by just the diagonal coverage gave models in all three (`general.log`). No conclusion from the caps. Keep a left shape.
- **G, first three steps.** (1) In `scratch/ideas/r018_N/`, run `sha256sum -c FREEZE.sha256`, `bash replay.sh control` and `bash replay.sh check`; independently audit the 17 roster maps, the 60,990 residual count and the binder before credit. (2) Price **one** of the two remaining `h=8` left shapes with the any-right, one-site formula. Stop on SAT, binding failure or 30 CPU-s; do not debit the other shape. (3) Before a full `h=7` run, price eight dispersed untested left-only contexts, including each board, nontrivial stabilizers and a transpose-asymmetric board. Include proof bytes and independent replay. Stop before bulk solving if any produces a model/cap or the measured forecast saves less than 10× against a counted fixed-alignment queue.
