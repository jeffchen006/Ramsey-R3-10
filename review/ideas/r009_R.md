# r009_R — join two Ramsey windows on the degrees of their common vertices

**Recommendation: one idea for C/D.** A complete bipartite graph on two triples gives *two* induced Ramsey graphs whose overlap has an exact degree requirement at every vertex. Use that requirement as a lookup key before placing a single unknown edge. The 22/22 pilot produced no surviving marked map at codegree cap six; the adjacent 22/21 pilot is promising at cap five. These are scout measurements, not case-status changes.

The full replay, including compilation, controls, independent generators, both samples and proof checking, took **57.28 s wall**, 54.49 user + 2.50 system CPU-s (`S/full-replay.log`, `S/full-replay.time`). ## 1. Reciprocal triples (rank 1; hand reduction, measured enum, restricted terminal certs)

**Cover and join key — proved.** Let independent triples A,B span a complete K₃,₃ in a hypothetical 9-regular graph. Write F_A for the graph on the vertices outside A and its open neighbourhood; define F_B symmetrically. Each has independence number at most six, since its independent sets can be adjoined to the defining triple. No vertex outside A+B sees both triples (triangle). Consequently F_A ∪ F_B contains exactly the other 34 vertices. Put Z = F_A ∩ F_B and Q_A = F_A − Z, Q_B = F_B − Z. Only edges between Q_A and Q_B remain unknown once the marked windows are matched. In particular,

```
|Z| = |F_A| + |F_B| − 34;
d_F_B(z) = 9 + d_Z(z) − d_F_A(z)  for every z in Z.
```

The neighbours of each b in B outside A form an independent six-set in F_A. The three six-sets cover precisely Q_A. Their pairwise overlaps are at most M−3 at codegree cap M, because their centers already share A. Multiplicities at a site are retained; a site may see all three. Thus to generate a half, enumerate three six-sets with the specified union size, not a gluing of a 30-vertex graph. The roles of the three sets can be unordered: permutations of A and B separately preserve their complete bipartite incidence. At M≤6 no two sets coincide.

For 22/22, |Z|=10, both unions have size 12. At M=5 the three pair overlaps must each be two and the triple overlap zero: inclusion–exclusion requires sum of pair overlaps minus the triple overlap to be six. No such equality assumption was made at M=6. For 22/21, |Z|=9 and the two unions have sizes 13 and 12, respectively.

**Index before joining.** In a half F, require the opposite degree at z to be in [0,6]. For x in Q with h chosen sets containing it, require `9−d_Q(x)−h ≤ 6` (its remaining neighbours lie in the other window and are independent), `d_F(x)+h ≤ 9`, and check codegree already spent. The lookup key is the sorted multiset of `(d_Z(z),d_F(z))`, refined by appending the sorted colours of the neighbours of z in Z. To request a mate, replace every degree colour d_F(z) by `9+d_Z(z)−d_F(z)` *before* applying the refinement. Equal keys are only candidates: enumerate all Z-isomorphisms with the required degrees. With an alignment fixed, nonedges between private sites sharing a known neighbour are compulsory. Insert every still possible cross-edge provisionally: an independent ten in this most generous graph kills the alignment irrespective of every remaining choice.

### 22/22: full local-source pilot, not a Ramsey conclusion

Source: all 191 order-22 (3,7) classes in `gluing/val/r3_7_22.g6`; one input per class, no assumption about automorphisms. Counts before alignment refer to *ordered* pairs and allow reuse of a host. A half is a specific unordered triple of masks on a source graph, not an unrooted 40-vertex graph.

| Stage | M=5 | M=6 (inclusive) |
|---|---:|---:|

| Distinct `(source graph,Z)` | 202,980 | 1,742,749 |
| Ordered marked pairs matching the degree multiset | 4 | 12,019 |
| After one neighbour-colour refinement | 0 | 1,905 |
| Exact aligned marked maps actually checked, modulo side exchange | 0 | 882 |

Two generators agree **byte-for-byte on every `(graph,Z,multiplicity)`**: `S/reciprocal.cpp` starts with six-sets; `S/corefirst.cpp` starts with every ten-subset. Python independently re-extracts the actual masks on every cap-six card that reaches alignment. At cap five a separate coloured-nauty index also gives zero mates (`S/index40.log`). The factor of roughly `6.6×10¹⁰` at cap six compares `11,198,732²` raw marked pairs to 1,905 pairs with equal fingerprints, **not** a claimed wall-time speed-up for all R(6).

At cap six, 863/882 maps already force an independent ten. For the other 19, exact row-degree choices plus forced edges suffice. If deleting a possible cross-edge from the upper graph makes an independent ten, that edge is mandatory; intersect row domains, forbid triangles and repeat. The raw map run does **not** quotient by a partial-graph automorphism. See `S/join6-basic.log` and `S/join6-prop.log`.

**Independent terminal check:** all 19 also give checked LRATs for separately generated formulas containing only degree, triangle and independent-ten clauses. No codegree lower bounds, codegree upper bounds or lane C path exclusions occur in these formulas. `S/verify_terminal.py`, `S/lrat6.log` and `S/terminal/map*.{cnf,lrat}`. The finite source/marking cover remains **enum**, not LRAT-certified. This says nothing about graphs lacking this marked pair of windows.

**Why it matters to the open blocks.** In R(5), if the three pairs of a triple A all have codegree five and A has exactly three common neighbours B, then |F_A|=22 by inclusion–exclusion. If the opposite triple has the same property, the pilot applies; the members of B need not be labelled in advance. In R(6), C's codegree-six P3 has |F_A|=22. For *any* three of its common neighbours B, let their six-set masks in F_A be M₁,M₂,M₃. Then `|F_B|=34−|M₁∪M₂∪M₃|`. Audit the result above to replace the elementary union lower bound 12 by **13**. It can be redundant: in C's diagonal-four 8/8 layouts, four masks already covering ≥20 vertices force each triple to cover ≥14. Don't spend cores adding a redundant cut there. A reciprocal-22 exclusion does not exclude the entire P3 or all triangles of codegree-five pairs.

### 22/21: measured extension, stream only after audit

At cap five, fix the full 22-side source and sample the order-21 side from `scratch/B/val/r3_7_21.g6` (1,118,436 rows). The full 22-side gives 7,791,069 local marks. Two *disjoint* 21-side tests, with exact isomorphisms and every remaining map processed:

| Order-21 input | Local marks | Marked pairs after colour lookup | Exact aligned role maps | Surviving maps |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Every 4,096th row, starting at zero (274 hosts) | 139,739 | 278 | 330 | 0 |
| Fixed-seed uniform sample of 1,024 other hosts | 503,582 | 8,663 | 10,964 | 0 |

There can be several isomorphisms per pair, hence the increase in column four. The held-out join took about 12 CPU-s; the 21-side mask generation about 1 CPU-s (`S/held-join.log`, `S/held21.time`). The first sample alone would understate join density by about 8× per host. Neither sample justifies declaring the unsampled hosts empty. The mathematical target for D is the next defect of a maximal-codegree triangle, *not* all R(5): when |F_A|=22, |F_B|=21 means the three opposite masks have union 13.

**Cost estimate and stop rule.** Extrapolating the 274- and 1,024-host mask-generation and alignment rates gives roughly 1–4 CPU-hours for those stages on the 21-side census; allow **1–6 CPU-hours**, not a guarantee, for a streaming run at cap five. Source validation and proof-producing fallback are additional. Pre-index the small 22-side once, and emit a 21-side card only if its requested key exists; don't materialize hundreds of millions of 21-side records. Kill expansion if an independent matched control is lost, an independent key replay disagrees, or a further timed 8,192-host tranche projects >20 CPU-hours / leaves >1% of aligned maps undecided by the cheap tests. Do not transfer the estimate to cap six or to 21/21.

**Calibration and novelty.** In the ten 6-regular (3,7;22) controls, use opposite *pairs* of roots spanning a square with both diagonals of codegree two. Their two order-10 windows overlap in two vertices; the identical degree identity holds with 6 in place of 9. All **334 directed-side marked occurrences** pass actual mask recovery, core alignment and propagation. Exact completion of one planted template from each class gave 29 labelled solutions and **exactly the ten known unrooted classes** (`S/models22.log`). The asymmetric control retained all **395** planted 10/11 occurrences over the same ten classes (`S/mixed-cal.log`). This is not D's punctured K₃,₃ root or C's one-sided P3 packing. C's earlier 12/14 window pre-join evaluated predicates *per map* for only 1.51× overall. Here the common vertices have no missing neighbours on either side, so the degree identity eliminates almost all pairs *before any map exists*.

**First three steps for lanes C/D.** (1) C/H: separately replay the opposite-window identity, the two half generators, 882 raw maps and the 19 terminal checks; retain the distinction between enum coverage and checked terminal proofs. (2) D: independently generate the asymmetric 10/11 control, then time 8,192 new cap-five hosts with a streaming degree index and record exact alignments, undecided fraction and end-to-end CPU. (3) Only if those gates pass, process the full 22/21 cap-five source with an input manifest and proof-backed survivors. C can use the audited 22/22 rule on relevant three-common-neighbour layouts without waiting for step 3.
