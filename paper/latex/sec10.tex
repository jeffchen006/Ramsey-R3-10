\section{Use of AI, and work in progress}\label{sec:ai}

\paragraph{How this paper was produced.} Most of this work was produced by AI. A team of AI agents (Claude Code, by
Anthropic) wrote the code, ran the computations, wrote the technical notes and wrote most of this text, under the
direction of the author. OpenAI Codex was used as a research assistant for ideas; the edge-kernel reduction
(Sec.~\ref{sec:hard-kernels}), the split by $q(v)$ and the other ideas listed in Sec.~\ref{sec:hard-kernels} came from it
(\code{notes/duo.md}). The author set the goals and chose the directions.

\paragraph{What ``CONFIRMED'' means.} The label CONFIRMED and the review numbers (``Review $n$'') refer to a separate AI
agent that re-derived each claim, where possible with its own code; its log is \code{notes/critic.md}. No human referee
has checked the hand proofs or the enumerations. The \textbf{certified} results do not depend on this review: they rest on
verified checkers (cake\_lpr, CakePB, the Lean kernel) and on the published facts of Sec.~\ref{sec:pub}. The author
does not guarantee results that are not marked as certified.
