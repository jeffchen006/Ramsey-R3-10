## R(7) certificate acceptance

The reported Lemma R7 and GR's complete e85 census give the four roots of the reduction, not the historical SAT cubes. Within a residue, A enumerates all independent masks of the necessary sizes, independently recomputes each side signature `(used, demand)`, and counts opposite sides with disjoint `used` and both containment constraints. Division by two is justified by nonempty disjoint unions. The submitted tuples are checked for membership and uniqueness modulo swapping within and between sides, so equality of counts verifies coverage. At each proof-tree pivot, every genuine extension needs a present colour in the support. The children try every support colour and partition completions by their first present one. Both capacity exhaustion and zero support for an unsatisfied MTF pair are valid closures. The replayer rejects truncated proofs, trailing bytes and any purported complete-choice node. No per-root timeout or leaf heuristic is an input to the checked result. Checked digests and per-seed totals are in `scratch/critic/r7-replay.log`; the lane's planted-root and missing-cover controls are in `scratch/A/rc7/audit-{control,negative,missing}.log`.
