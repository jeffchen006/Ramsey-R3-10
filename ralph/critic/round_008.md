## Fourth-round deep audit: C-004, the full regular max-codegree-seven exclusion

I selected this claim because it closes an entire value of the maximum codegree, superseding a 99-open-cube barrier. Round 004 audited the degree-six floor; this is a different dependency chain. **CONFIRMED, hand reduction + finite cover + custom tree certificate.** Not a new LRAT or a proof of the historical 99 cubes.

1. **From a graph to four inputs.** By Lemma R7 (`paper/latex/sec3.tex`), the two exclusive pairs induce K2,2 and the 27-vertex remainder X has 85 edges and alpha at most seven. The lower-edge and four-class premises are GR Table 13, D-E8/D-C8e in `notes/DEPENDENCIES.md`. I separately filtered the bulk graph6 stream of 477,142 graphs at ≤85 edges, canonically labelled the output, and compared it with the frozen input: exactly the same four. Seed validation checks the labelled adjacencies, not just their counts. This cross-check is not a new proof of GR's census.
2. **Necessity, not a maximality shortcut.** Each exclusive vertex contributes an independent 6-mask in X, each common vertex an independent 7-mask. All four cross edges force the two exclusive-side unions disjoint. If x misses a degree-nine neighbourhood and is nonadjacent to its centre, x extends that neighbourhood to an I10. Thus domination follows already in this regular case. Every x hits a common neighbour or would have to hit both exclusive sides, forming a triangle. If x has no common neighbour in X with a non-neighbour on an exclusive side, the opposite side must witness the pair. Within-side overlap has cap `7−3`; common-common overlap has cap `7−2`. These justify the package filters and residual-degree rows in `ralph/lanes/C/proofs/pair_packing.md`. There is no assumption that X is MTF.
3. **Coverage and tree semantics.** Read `ralph/lanes/A/r7_audit.cpp`. The side checker generates the eligible masks, counts compatible side pairs independently of the submitted tuples, checks membership and rejects duplicates after exchange within/between sides. Nonempty disjoint side unions justify halving the oriented count; no X automorphism quotient is required. In the tree, a pivot has positive residual vertex demand or is an unwitnessed required pair. The children cover every permitted mask containing it: the *first chosen* such mask places a completion in one child. Earlier choices are excluded among siblings; demand, overlap, and disjoint-mask budgets are otherwise updated in child-local state. A leaf must show too few available masks for a demand or none for a required pair. An independent seven-set plus avoiding common vertices and the larger avoiding exclusive side explains its avoidance budget. A complete choice, bad pivot, early EOF or trailing data is not a successful proof.
4. **Executable and falsification.** From critic scratch I rebuilt A's independent checker against the frozen code and nine input hashes. In input order: **261,824 / 261,586 / 226,943 / 226,655** exact covers and trees; total **977,008 trees, 49,569,079 nodes**. All consumed exactly. On the known C22 example the actual tuple `(7,2096;648,1344)` passes membership, a false root closure fails, and that single valid tuple passed as a full cover also fails (123 needed). `scratch/critic/r8/R/{run.log,controls.log,planted.log}` and `R/full/replay-X*.log`.
