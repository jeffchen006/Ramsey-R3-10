# Folded two-defect physical boards (hand)

## Scope and complete cover

Work only under the accepted adjacent degree-six equality premises at `e=170` (G-043, `e170_equality_signatures.md`). The two roots have five further neighbours each, the sole wing crossing is `L0–R0`, and `W` has 28 vertices, each with one or two neighbours on each wing. Each wing has four private vertices at each anchor and eight double vertices, with distinct double masks. Put `h=|D_L ∩ D_R|`. Inclusion-exclusion gives `|W−(D_L∪D_R)|=12+h`, `|D_L−D_R|=|D_R−D_L|=8−h`, so at `h=6` there are 18 grid copies, two singleton/double sites each way, and six double/double sites. Every board entry is a multiplicity, not a Boolean. On each shore, the two singleton slots are the deficits `4−margin`; a repeated deficit uses **two distinct slots** even with identical contact masks. Each of the ten double masks gets one slot, eight of which must be occupied. The marked crossing prevents any support containing `L0,R0`; in particular `M[0,0]=0`. The respective crossed anchor has root+crossing+four private neighbours already, so at most three occupied double slots involve it. Hence the decorated cover need only consider the thirty hole lists per shore.

For a row of sum `m` and `p` nonempty **noncross** cells, the private-cell matching from G-043 to the opposite noncross anchors has size at most `p+(4−m)`: each grid copy can meet only its own opposite anchor, and each non-grid private vertex contributes at most once to the matching. Its lower bound is `3+[row=0]`. Thus `m≤4` and `m−p≤1−[row=0]`, with the same on columns. In particular cross entries are at most one and noncross entries are at most two. Exhaust these weak bounds and the total of 18, then quotient by independent noncross `S4` labels on both sides and by root exchange. These are coordinate changes; no graph automorphism is claimed. For a fixed representative, quotient the pair of hole lists only by the actions fixing the *multiplicity board*. A class counts a weak hole-context key, not an alignment and not a known realizable board.

## Edge binding and necessary rows

For each legal pair of labelled left/right slots, use a selector. Each occupied slot has exactly one selected mate, each unoccupied slot none; all singleton slots are occupied. Exclude singleton/singleton matches (they belong to the grid). The selected matching is thus an injective description of the same physical non-grid vertices from two shores. Grid-to-grid edges share a variable between shores and are omitted if any support overlaps. Each shore has bits for every other pair with disjoint **same-shore** supports, and true bits imply both endpoint slots occupied. For each selected mate, equate the two descriptions of its edge to every grid copy. For every two disjoint selected mates, equate both descriptions of their mutual edge. If one shore omits a bit, equality is to false: same-shore support overlap would form a triangle with the common anchor. Every physical option/option edge of a real graph is thus accounted for, rather than set to false. Triangles on `W` need only be encoded on the left physical coordinates: each active site is represented exactly once there. Triangles touching the boundary are ruled out by the crossing and shared-support omissions.

For the private row at anchors `i,j` on a shore, a total of six chosen private vertices already pads to ten using the remaining four neighbours of their root (the remaining three anchors and the other root). Choose five instead if the `ij` double slot is occupied, since that site shares an anchor with each private vertex. The chosen cells themselves have no edges within a cell. The row therefore requires at least one edge across the chosen subsets, guarded by activity of the double slot in the five-site case. It uses all four actual private vertices per anchor, including both labelled copies in a repeated deficit. For width `w` and forbidden independent size `k` in the controls, the two cut sizes become `k−w+1` and `k−w`, respectively.

For every empty noncross grid cell, maximality requires a common neighbour of its two anchors, and no boundary vertex can witness. Every admissible selector whose masks cover the anchors is in the witness clause; nonempty cells already have a grid witness. For a grid copy or a selected pair at a missing boundary anchor, maximality also requires a common neighbour. Neither root meets any exterior vertex. The only possible boundary witness is the opposite endpoint of the crossing, when the anchor is crossed and the exterior site meets that opposite endpoint. Unless this holds, use the disjunction of *all* same-coordinate edge bits to grid copies or slots meeting the anchor, with the selector guard for a non-grid site. An unoccupied slot cannot spuriously witness because true incident edges imply occupancy. The small controls omit MTF by design.

In the target both degree-six residues have minimum degree at least six (G-019 as used in G-043). For exterior support sizes `(a,b)`, the two residue degrees are `d_W+b` and `d_W+a`, and the graph degree is `d_W+a+b≤9`. Therefore `6−min(a,b)≤d_W≤9−a−b`. Use shared physical edge bits and guard each slot's counter by the disjunction of all mate selectors yielding its sizes. Inactive slots have false edges. `full` appends only these degree counters, using sequential cardinality encodings; optional `budget` additionally has the accepted 86-edge equality, but no `budget` formula enters the baseline queue.

Finally a positive padded blocker uses a set of grid copies and at most one selected non-grid pair in target mode. If all its edge literals are false and its selector guard (if any) activates the named pair, the indicated different physical sites are independent. The binder may pad with other distinct grid copies sharing an anchor pairwise and with every chosen site; all those adjacencies are forced absent. Add an independent boundary subset whose anchors avoid all these supports. The boundary is exactly both roots, both wings and their one crossing, and roots have no exterior neighbours. If the witness reaches ten vertices, the blocker is necessary. Omitting other independent-set or maximality clauses makes the target encoding a relaxation, so SAT alone is not a graph verdict. In the smaller exact controls the generator instead covers **all** independent subsets via unrestricted numbers of selected pairs with distinct slots and supports; early stopping merely skips supersets of an already blocked independent set.

For an optional split at a specified double slot, occupancy is Boolean and each real labelled placement belongs to exactly one of its two unit polarities. To close a whole representative by this split both complementary formulas must check unless a wider receipt already covers it. Do not subtract an entire hole-context key on a partially covered split unless its coverage follows independently from the key's fixed occupancy.
