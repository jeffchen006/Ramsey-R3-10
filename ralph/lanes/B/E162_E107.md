## Hand reduction

Let `v,w` be the degree-seven vertices. The neighbor-degree sum at `v`
is `56 + |N(v) ∩ V9| - [vw is an edge]`, and the floor gives
`Z(v) = 162 - e(G-N[v]) <= 55`. Thus `vw` is an edge, `v` has no
degree-nine neighbor, and `Z(v)=55`. The same holds for `w`. At root
`v`, `H=G-N[v]` has order 32, size 107, is triangle-free, and has
`alpha(H)<=8`. Among the seven rows of attachments to `H`, the row
belonging to `w` has size 6 and the other six rows have size 7.

Each row is an independent dominating set in `H`. It is independent by
triangle-freeness. If it misses `x∈H`, maximality gives a common neighbor
of `x` and its row vertex in `H`: no vertex in `N(v)` is adjacent to the
row vertex, and the root is not adjacent to `x`. Moreover, any pair of
vertices at distance at least three in `H` (including disconnected pairs)
must lie together in a row, since maximality requires a common neighbor
outside `H` and the root cannot be that neighbor. A pair that lies in no
independent dominating set of sizes 6 or 7 therefore rules out this root.
