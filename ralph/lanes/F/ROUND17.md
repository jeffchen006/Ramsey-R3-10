## A new local result: a 28-vertex interval forces an independent nine (hand + cert)

Number any fourteen consecutive untouched sites locally 0..13. A simple matching occurs precisely at absolute differences 6..12. Its **nonedge sign** is the XOR of endpoint bits on either unmatched pair. A support admits an independent singleton transversal exactly when the nonedge signs sum to zero around every cycle of its induced crossing graph. Complementing all endpoint bits gives a second transversal. Triangle-freeness in the physical lift forces even nonedge parity on each quotient triangle. None of this uses a vertical.

Consider quotient triangles `(0,6,12)` and `(1,7,13)` joined by the matching `(0,7), (6,13), (12,1)`. They form a triangular prism. The three lateral squares sum, as edge sets modulo two, to the two triangles. **At least one square has even nonedge parity.** For each square there is a nine-site support with cycle space generated by that square and one triangle:

| square | support | remaining cycle |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7–1–12–0 | 0..7,12 | 0–6–12–0 |
| 0–6–13–7–0 | 0..7,13 | 1–7–13–1 |
| 1–12–6–13–1 | 1,6..13 | 1–7–13–1 |

Whichever square is even gives a balanced nine-site support. In particular, **the untouched 34-card always contains an independent nine**, without an assumption about the exceptional row or the four poles. This does not assert an independent ten. The conventional proof of this 14-site local statement has just 48 variables, 76 clauses, three supports and a 774-byte LRAT. `marked_nine.py cert` reconstructs it from three frozen masks without the pilot.
