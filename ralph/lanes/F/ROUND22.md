## From a footprint to an actual 36-card

A double crossing is a complete bipartite 2-by-2. A vertical at either end would form a triangle. Thus a doubled **marked** row permits the two exceptional endpoints in one independent set. To pad them by eight ordinary singletons, exclude the **whole** occupied footprint. Around a quotient triangle, triangle-freeness forces the XOR of the three nonedge signs to vanish. More generally, a singleton support has an independent transversal exactly if all its cycle parities vanish. This finds obstructions beyond the old induced-forest sufficient condition. On a bare card only, we can add every vertical not incident to a double: nobody else on the card meets both endpoints at such a site. This only makes independent tens harder to find, and does not claim the edge was initially present. A positive saturated card is an actual graph, but a different signing or omitted vertical may be needed for further extensions.

`exception_sign.py` seeds up to twelve cheapest supports using **both** exceptional endpoints on doubled rows only. On undoubled rows it does not use this seed or pad by that pair. Separation then uses actual independent tens, possibly including both ends at other forced holes. No translations/rotations of the marked row are used. Forest normalization is independent endpoint relabelling in the signed quotient, not an assumed graph automorphism beyond the fixed card structure. `cyclic_check.py` rebuilds the physical exclusions, validates the full cycle space for each support, checks the forest switch normalization, reconstructs CNF and runs both LRAT consumers. On SAT, the 36-vertex graph passes the graph gate; a separate reader binds physical site degrees and mirror incidences to the exact marked row.

## An extension certificate does not freeze a SAT seed

`exception_extend.py` starts again with **all signs free**. Every vertical not forced absent is a Boolean, as are all edges incident to the new vertices. Each ordinary/exceptional matching has one sign: complementary endpoint parities get complementary edge literals; a double has four constant edges. The initial clauses forbid all physical triangles and normalize signs only on a forest. Given any trial assignment, an actual independent ten supplies the disjunction of its physical edge literals. Seed order-36 I9s are used to suggest physical ten-sets, never to constrain the signing to that seed. The core involution can suggest another ten-set even if the poles break that symmetry: only its unconditional physical edge clause is used. UNSAT of any such finite subset refutes the whole signed branch.

`exception_bind.py` independently derives the switch forest by incidence rank, constructs every physical edge term anew, and regenerates each clause from ten distinct vertex labels. The input CNF must match bytewise at first binding, and **both `lrat-check` and `cake_lpr` must accept the rebuilt CNF**. Packed proofs retain the physical deck and LRAT losslessly; the CNF is regenerated on replay. With a pole present there is **no** assumption that ordinary or exceptional verticals can be added. A seven-vertex positive explicitly has its pole adjacent to both ends of an undoubled marked site; inserting that vertical produces a triangle. `plain_pole_ctl.stdout`, `plain-pole.gate`. Smaller generic control: a signed 8-core from C13(1,5) extends to a gated (3,5;13) graph, but to no (3,5;14) graph for any of its signings. C8(1,4) and the order-nine obstruction at target four test the undoubled adapter separately.
