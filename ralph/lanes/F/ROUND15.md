## The requested missing-vertical test

Name site zero and delete its vertical. Pick both its endpoints. A further eight singleton sites must come from its ten nonneighbors, giving exactly 45 possible supports. On a quotient edge `ij` write `x_ij` for the endpoint difference giving a *nonedge*. Independent singleton choices obey `b_i+b_j=x_ij`. Solve separately on each connected component: there are two endpoint orientations per component if all of its cycle sums vanish, and none otherwise. Triangle-freeness sets the crossing sums on quotient triangles to zero.

One support, `{1,2,3,4,14,15,16,17}`, has just one cycle. Its six-cycle must have odd sum in a strict card with the named hole. This already cuts 26 of the 88,469 R13-labelled first-column footprints compatible with the *exactly-one-hole* mark (a matching-independent column cannot hit one endpoint at every site of this cycle). The direct joint degree-four indicator test under this single parity **did not** refute the hole. Proof did not stop at that unsuccessful span test: all 45 events together depend on only seven cycle parities of the ten-site neighborhood. These seven forms are independent even after enforcing the original triangle constraints. Of the 128 values, 117 guarantee a ten with both endpoints of site zero and only 11 avoid all 45 supports. This projects a local neighborhood; it never visits the 22-dimensional global switching space.

For the 11 local values, insert only their seven affine equations and examine ten-*singleton* supports. Rank zero is an independent ten; at rank one the unique equation must fail to avoid a ten, hence its complement is necessary. Repeating leaves nine contradictions of rank zero. The two remaining values (79,107 in the saved chart) have respectively 22 and 18 support indicators whose Boolean XOR is constant one. `hole_verify.py` reconstructs these events afresh in original edge and literal endpoint coordinates, using a different edge order and no switching gauge. This proves a missing vertical at zero is impossible, and hence at any site by relabeling the unsigned circulant. No individual signing is assumed rotation invariant.

## Why this also excludes the restored profile

The 117 local values first discarded for producing a *full-hole* ten must be reconsidered when the vertical is restored. We did so separately, using **no hole supports**. Every one now ends in a rank-zero ten-singleton contradiction after affine implications. Together the 128 records use 1,421 support occurrences but only **297 distinct ten-site masks**. The piecewise argument is thus unnecessary for the final certificate: a strict card would avoid independent transversals on each of these 297 masks simultaneously. The whole CNF for this necessary condition is UNSAT.

`hole_lrat.py` builds a single 1,386-variable / 5,702-clause CNF from the masks. It sets nonedge parities to zero on a quotient forest by permissible per-site endpoint flips, adds the triangle equations, and requires at least one odd cycle on every listed ten-site mask. The independent checker `cyclic_check.py` reconstructs all forest/triangle/XOR clauses and verifies on each mask that the listed forms span exactly its cycle space. The 125,394-byte textual LRAT is accepted by both `lrat-check` and `cake_lpr`. A frozen regeneration in a separate stem, from just the distance set and archived masks, produced byte-identical CNF and LRAT. Thus any ID21 lift has an independent transversal on at least one of these masks, and also its distinct global mate. This is a score lower bound, not a measured optimum.
