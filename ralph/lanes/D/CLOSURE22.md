# A 17-pentagon path with two deficiencies has at most 14 untouched edges

**Submitted: hand + enum + cert (local CNFs).** In regular R(3), assume exactly two deficient nonedges, choose either unique-witness path `a-h-b`, and suppose exactly 17 edges cross its private wings. Then `m=e(Y)≤14`. This excludes the *whole marked m=15 layer*, not a kernel or all such paths. The old `m=16` exclusion is D-044. No graph candidate.

## What the 149 receipts prove

The R-007 projection keeps all seven poles `X=N(h)−{a,b}`, all fourteen `Y` vertices and **all eight** vertices of the short private wing `A`, deleting all eight of `B`. Number vertices `X,Y,A` in this order. The independent-set caps are **7 on X+Y** (pad with both tips) and **8 on X+Y+A** (pad with `b`). A witness card is a physical set of 8 or 9 vertices in those respective domains; the independent binder emits the disjunction of **every** edge internal to that set.

All repeated supports are distinct vertices. The fixed Y order is triple first, doubles in lexicographic pole-pair order with repeats. `X` and `A` are separately independent. Every pair has at most three common neighbors; within either `X` or `A` the bound inside the projection is two, since `h` or `a` is outside. Each Y sees 2–3 vertices on each private wing. Every A-site has 2–3 B neighbors. These floor bounds use D-028: the other defective path is disjoint from this one including its middle. Thus `6≤|S_y|+d_Y(y)+d_A(y)≤7` and `5≤|P_w|+d_Y(w)≤6`. The 13 pole contacts of the short wing give five double and three singleton rows; equal ranks may be sorted in decreasing binary order. No individual A mask is pinned; ties do not identify distinct vertices. `e(A,Y)=8·8−17−13=34`. These are necessary constraints, not a test that any relaxed model extends.

For O, after labelling the two exceptional poles 0 and 1, designate as A the shore deficient at pole 0; there is no claim that this is an automorphism. In D either shore can be retained. Conditioned proofs do not divide their assignment count by a shore exchange. In every case all eight short vertices are present; `e(Y)=14` rather than 15.
