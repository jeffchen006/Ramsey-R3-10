# The single-witness branch of R(3)

**Result (`hand + enum`, submitted for review).** The branch is empty. Every hypothetical R(3) graph admits two vertex-disjoint deficient nonedges with **different** unique common neighbors. These neighbors need not both be external to the four endpoints.

Let D be the graph of unordered codegree-one pairs; assume every such pair has the same unique common neighbor z. This is a condition on **every** deficient pair in the candidate graph, not merely the two pairs chosen for rooting. Then D is a matching on N(z), hence has at most four edges. `DEFECT_EXCESS.md` gives the elementary parity refinement to 2 or 4. The graph searches below do not rely on the parity refinement, a forest, a general C6/C8 ban, or a preselected placement of z.

Base engine `witness_graph.cpp`: propagate mandatory and possible ordinary graph edges, exact degree, required common-neighbor bounds, triangle-freeness, and the independence-number bound as in `DEFECT_MATCHING.md`. In mode 16385, cap the number of deficient pairs by four and local degree in D by one. Each forced nonedge with at most one possible common neighbor either has none (contradiction) or has a unique compulsory center. All such centers must agree. When a common witness for a different nonedge is mandatory outside the remaining center possibilities, or no remaining center can be a witness for it, require two common neighbors for that nonedge. No vertex is treated as the sole center on evidence of just one known common neighbor when others are still possible. Whole-row branching is only quotiented by permutations of cells with identical fixed-star incidence. Node cap is always unresolved. Source and cutoff order for every stage bound by `witness_bind.py` in `scratch/D/wit11/<stage>/{receipt.tsv,live.tsv,bind.log}`.

**Dense roots.** D-019 provides an induced kernel P8 somewhere, with induced P7s at its two ends. Begin with all 168,540 generally live P7s (`scratch/D/joint9/all-live.tsv`). No path cap enters the ordinary stages. The two joins enumerate whole star profiles, not just the induced path edges. Each join removes the actual unique extra tip from each parent row, checks equality of shared cell sizes, then chooses per-cell overlaps with fixed marginals. At P8, nonadjacent tips have 1..3 external common neighbors; at P9 they already share the root on their side, so allow 0..2 additional. If two vertices see both tips and also two common frozen coordinates, the codegree cap is exceeded. All retained parent orientations are used. Each full join is checked by a second, independent count of overlap multisets and by reconstructing **both end deletions** of each child and checking orbit uniqueness under reversal. An intermediate audit initially failed at P9 because it did not swap the roots when reversing the even parent; the producer was unchanged, and the corrected checker agrees on both C22 and target (`witness_odd_audit.py`).

**Why this improves the unrestricted root.** D-020 gives disjoint D-edges ab,cd. If they have the same witness z, choose a D-edge of a different color (now established to exist), where color means the unique witness. If it hits both ab and cd, both its endpoints belong to N(z), giving it witness z, contradiction. Hence it is disjoint from at least one of ab and cd. This proves the result.
