## Common equations

Each wing site has one neighbor a or b, two wing neighbors and six Q neighbors. Seven members of Q form `Y=N(h)-{a,b}`. They are independent; a,b see no Q. There are `34` edges internal to the book, `7+16·6=103` from book to Q, hence `e(Q)=180-34-103=43`. Write `y(q)=1[q∈Y]`. For each q, `d_Q(q)+c(a,q)+c(b,q)=9+y(q)`, so `3+y(q) ≤ d_Q(q) ≤ 5+y(q)`. The true codegrees with h are `1+|N_Q(v)∩Y|` for a wing site v, and `|N_Q(q)∩Y|` for q outside Y. These nonedges have codegree two or three: h is not active by D-028. Two external vertices sharing a fixed contact cannot be adjacent. Every nonedge has codegree ≤3 and all but one of the pairs other than {a,b} must have codegree ≥2. Any subset of Q independent together with a boundary seed must have combined size ≤9.

For a selected wing patch P, each q has a mask `M_q=N(q)∩P`. If both wing neighbors of v are in P, the common neighbors of q and v are exactly its wing neighbors in `M_q` and the Q-neighbors of q whose masks contain v. If wing neighbors are missing, subtract their number **only from the required lower bound**: a true codegree upper bound still applies to the visible common neighbors. On an entire component no neighbor is missing. Pairwise conditions within the wing include the common endpoint for same-shore sites and every already known common **wing** neighbor. All formulas using only partial stars relax the omitted contributions; no missing neighbor is assumed absent.

## C16: an eight-site arc avoiding the exceptional endpoints

At most two sites of C16 are endpoints of the second weak pair. If both lie on it, they have opposite shore colors, since otherwise their common witness is a or b, contradicting D-028. Their two cyclic distances are odd and sum to 16, so the longer is at least 9. Its interior contains **eight consecutive vertices avoiding both**. With ≤1 endpoint on C16 the result is immediate. This does not require that the unique second witness avoids the chosen arc. We can rotate/reflect the C16 so the arc occupies sites `0..7`; fix the contact population on its central six `1..6`.
