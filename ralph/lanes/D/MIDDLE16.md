## What a true graph would supply (hand)

Follow scout R's notation in `review/ideas/r015_R.md`. Work only at K=2, t=17, m=16: the graph is in R(3), its two codegree-one paths are disjoint even including their middles (D-028), and at the marked path a-h-b exactly 17 edges join the private wings A,B. The 14 vertices Y outside the three closed neighborhoods span 16 edges. The seven poles X are the other neighbors of h. By regular degree balance the 21 vertices X+Y span 44 edges, so X sends 28 edges to Y. Each x has at most two neighbors on either wing (the common-neighbor upper bound three with the tip already counts h). Since x has degree nine including h, every x hits Y four times, A twice and B twice. No (h,y) is deficient and h has no neighbor outside a,b,X, so the 28 edges hit each y twice. The Y sites are thus physical edges of a loopless seven-pole multigraph, degree four and multiplicity at most two (pole pairs already share h).

Each wing vertex has one or two poles: its codegree with h counts its tip and cannot be deficient. The sum on its wing is 14; this makes six two-pole sites and two singletons. A six-edge multiset on the poles with degrees ≤2 specifies that entire wing's supports: add as many singletons per pole as needed to make degree two. For each pole pair, the Y,A,B multiplicity sum is one or two. Zero would create a second deficient pair with h as witness, forbidden by D-028. Triangle-freeness makes any mutually intersecting family of Y supports independent. If it covers fewer poles than its size, it extends with both tips and all unused poles to an independent ten. This last obstruction excludes a support without considering its variable edges.

## Coverage and terminal binding (enum)

`middle16_roster.py` recursively builds all upper triangles of the multiplicity matrix with sum four on each line. The 33 scout representatives partition the actual 52,065 labelled matrices by explicit permutation and set subtraction. An intersecting-family DFS rejects 27; six survive. Generating six nondecreasing pair indices from scratch gives 6,202 unconstrained shore words. For each left word, a bitset intersection over **all 21** pole pairs identifies every possible right word. The join includes equal words (zero legal diagonals, not assumed). Exact orbit sets under simultaneous pole permutations preserving Y and unrestricted swap of the two wings cover 320,878 ordered pairs, with 15,282 disjoint orbits. Orbit weight here is for a **fixed labelled Y support on the seven poles**, not its number of pole labellings. Common support does not identify physical sites: all copies get distinct residual rows. Permuting such copies to a fixed order is a relabelling; no residual adjacencies have been stipulated.

`S/roster.jsonl` contains every representative and weight. In host order 17,18,19,26,31,32, the orbit counts are 2,158/1,967/1,372/1,146/4,147/4,492. The two old tails (original 19:6127 and 32:8059; new canonical IDs 19:383 and 32:2367) have additional weights 96 and 28. Their CNFs use the old orientation, not necessarily the canonical representative. The 48 old holdouts were separately replayed as **enum only**.

Every produced ordinary CNF was independently reconstructed by `middle16_cnf.py`, compared byte-for-byte, then both `lrat-check` and `cake_lpr` checked the producer's LRAT against the reconstruction. No SMS symmetry lemmas. The independent binder builds explicit neighborhoods for h,a,b,X, folds constants and identifies residual nonedges by shared fixed neighbors. It encodes 9-regularity, the 16 and 17 subtotals, triangles and codegree bounds around the fixed stars. A second deficiency on a fixed/moving pair can only be between a pole and a residual vertex. At most one such debit is allowed; it is not required. Residual pairs with ≥2 already fixed witnesses cap additional common neighbors. For every independent subset of Y its pole neighborhood must be at least its size: the binder generates candidates from *pole subsets* and minimizes them, rather than using the scout's dependent-subset recurrence. The two implementations share the ordinary sequential-counter primitive, not the graph traversal or Hall compiler. These constraints relax graph existence. There is no converse, and local UNSAT is not a Section-7 proof for undivided R(3).

Control: all ten 6-regular (3,7,22) classes considered, three of codegree cap three; all 125 actual marked paths satisfy the pinned CNF, and flipping a residual bit refutes 125/125. This calibrates the graph-to-literal and general constraint binding with the smaller graph's actual counts and debit budget, not the target-only no-second-deficiency premises. The two original tail CNFs **and LRATs** were reproduced byte-for-byte.
