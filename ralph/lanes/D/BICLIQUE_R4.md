# The saturated R(4) K3,4 stratum remains open

In the equality split of `SATURATED_BICLIQUE.md`, write `U=U_0+U_1+U_2`
for the fifteen exclusive attachments to the small part, with independent
groups of size five, and `V` for the eighteen large-part attachments.
`V` has single-support groups `T_j` of size three and one double-support
site for each pair of the four large-part vertices. These conclusions
are conditional on an actual K3,4 in R(4), not forced for all R(4).

## Additional necessary structure (`hand`)

In `U`, choose one perfect matching between each pair of groups (the
matchings exist by the independence bound and König). Label two of them
as the identity from `U_0`. The third gives a permutation of five
labels; a fixed point would give a triangle. Its cycle type is therefore
either `5` or `2+3`, and the selected edges form a spanning `C15` or
`C6+C9`, respectively. A graph may realize both, so these matching
witnesses are a cover and not a disjoint split.

For an individual `T_j`, its three vertices plus the other three
large-part vertices are independent. They have no edges into `U` except
from `T_j`, so `U-N(T_j)` has alpha at most three, hence at most eight
vertices. In particular `|N_U(T_j)| >= 7`. Also for any two vertices of
`T_j` the analogous residual has alpha at most four. These are stronger
than checking only the sizes of `U,V` and the fixed incidences. They do
not give an obstruction by themselves; e.g. mere edge count is not tight.
Counting residual degrees gives `2e(U)+e(U,V)=120` and
`2e(V)+e(U,V)=138`, hence `e(V)=e(U)+9`.

## Exact finite check of the U-only constraints (`enum`)

Checked the 64,732 distinct order-15 `(3,6)` graphs regenerated by B from
the order-11 seed census (`ralph/lanes/B/src/bootstrap.sh`) and compared
to the historical class count there. Canonical dedup independently
returns 64,732 (`scratch/D/balanced15.canon.log`). The alternate E download with
1,592 entries is not an all-graphs census. The used archive is at
`scratch/B/val/r3_6_15.g6`, SHA-256
`9cd20155677af5fa130284a150f82cf5e99087771b4dc780dfe04e0459a9040e`.

`balanced3.cpp` checks triangle-freeness and alpha at most five, then
tests partitions into three independent fives by fixing vertex 0 in
the first group and the lowest remaining vertex in the second group.
For each partition it enforces the induced cap four on all codegrees,
cap three for pairs in the same group (they share the core anchor),
degree into each group at most four (the codegree with that anchor),
and total induced degree at most eight (each U vertex needs its anchor).
It does not attempt to extend an output to V and does not test the
T_j constraints above. The output is a necessary anchor set only.

Calibration on the complete 12-vertex `(3,5)` class list: independent
Python subset replay agrees on all 30 partitions, from 11 of the 12
graphs (`scratch/D/balanced12.replay`). Target: 48,322 of 64,732 have a
balanced partition, and 36,039 have at least one surviving partition
(67,730 surviving partitions total) with the anchor codegree rows;
1.19 CPU-s, `scratch/D/balanced15.log` and `balanced15.tsv`.
Thus this check does **not** close the K3,4 stratum or any R(4) kernel.
