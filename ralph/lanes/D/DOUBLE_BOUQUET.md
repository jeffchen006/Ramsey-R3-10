# Four pentagons on each of two disjoint deficient paths (round 13)

**Submitted: hand + enum + cert (local).** In every hypothetical R(3) graph there is a choice of two vertex-disjoint deficient paths so that each has **four** induced pentagon closures, and the sixteen new vertices of those eight closures are all distinct and outside both paths. The result gives a 22-site cover, **not eight disjoint pentagons**, and does not require the four chosen edges of a book to extend to one perfect matching. A graph can have many such markings. This neither decides R(3) nor certifies an unrooted Ramsey exclusion.

**Separate root selection result (same labels).** If old four-star ID 179 occurs, some disjoint marking has a nonadjacent cross endpoint pair of codegree **at most two**. Therefore **101** four-star representatives suffice instead of 102. The marking can be chosen to admit the four-plus-four packing as well. This is not an exclusion of ID 179 as an internal marking of a graph.

Evidence directory `S=scratch/D/join13`; original IDs are zero-based line numbers in `scratch/D/ind10/four-40.in`. The input cover is the confirmed D-028. The four local CNFs have ordinary labelled LRATs; the use of D-028, the 102-key binding, positive certificates and semantic reductions below still matter. No SMS symmetry clause is used in the local formulas.

## 1. Books on fixed physical sites

For a deficient pair `a,b` with its unique common neighbor `h`, the wings `A=N(a)-h`, `B=N(b)-h` are independent disjoint eight-sets; `h` touches neither. Every wing edge is an induced pentagon closure. The wings have a perfect matching by Hall (otherwise an independent nine in their union, plus `h`). Work with the *same two paths* and the *same physical labels* in every extension. Every disjoint pair of deficient paths in an R(3) graph has one of the 102 restricted four-star types: enumerate the four complete endpoint stars, disallow a directly forced independent ten, and require distinct witnesses outside all four endpoints. This assertion about already-disjoint paths does not rule out other types of marks elsewhere. Existence of a disjoint pair is precisely where D-028 is needed.

On **91** roots there are disjoint wing pools `W0,W1`, both avoiding both paths. Each has a known independent seed `T` disjoint and anticomplete to that pool, with `|T|+|W|>=13`. The minimum vertex cover in the induced bipartite graph on W has size at least four: otherwise W minus the cover, together with T, is an independent ten. König gives four matched edges. They need not extend to a perfect matching. `S/seed-roots-4.jsonl` stores the two explicit pool/seed masks per positive. `book_audit.py` verifies every required absence from only a frozen star or a triangle through a frozen endpoint, independently of the seed finder.

On **eight** more roots take *any* perfect matching of each book and remove petals touching the opposite path. A petal is its two new sites. A remaining left/right petal pair conflicts if these sets intersect. The conflict graph has maximum degree two. The number of conflict edges is at most the number E of shared eligible physical sites, calculated from the four frozen stars. Retain `l,r` petals (lower bounds suffice), orient so `r>=8`. If at least `l-4` left vertices have degree two, omit `l-4` of them, leaving at most `E-2(l-4)` incident conflict edges. Otherwise the four least left degrees are at most one each. In either case four opposite petals remain available when `E<=2(l-4)+r-4`. This direct arithmetic covers IDs **114,115,116,117,118,120,121,122**. No component-class census is needed for the theorem.

## 2. Only three local difficulties

IDs **110,119,179** each have exactly one blocked site in each book (in the opposite path), so seven petals survive per side. On ID 110 at most seven sites belong to both eligible wing pools; on 119 at most eight. On 179 there are nine, but the opposite blocked sites are the endpoints of the single cross edge `0--2`. The mate of 2 in the first matching is in `N(1)∩N(2)`; the mate of 0 in the second is in `N(0)∩N(3)`. These are distinct, shared eligible sites, both lost from the overlap of the surviving matchings. Thus there are at most **seven** conflict edges on 179. No assumption of K=2 in these bounds.

In all three cases the conflict graph on 7+7 has at most eight edges. If some left petal has degree zero, add three of the remaining lowest-degree left petals: their degree sum is at most three (either the three discarded largest all have degree two, or the three smallest all have degree at most one). At least four right petals remain. Thus failure of four-plus-four implies no isolated petal on either side, hence at most one degree-two petal per side. Let `U_i` be the eligible wing sites (the 15 sites outside the opposite path). For the actual mate `b_i` of the blocked site, the support of the seven surviving petals is exactly `U_i-{b_i}`. Then

`T_i = U_i - (U_(1-i) - {b_(1-i)})`

**must be independent** if four-plus-four fails. Indeed an edge of `T_i` is a wing edge avoiding every opposite surviving petal; at most two same-side matching petals meet it. At least six original petals on that side have conflict degree one, so choose three of those avoiding the new edge. The four chosen edges then conflict with at most three of the opposite seven. This proof is why single, unaligned ten-site interface checks alone do not suffice.

`book_last.py` emits all nine ordered mate choices for each of the three roots. The independent `book_audit.py` checks the actual option sets and claimed absences. On ID 110 each choice gives a named forced independent ten (in particular the eight purely private sites of the first book can be padded by the two opposite endpoints). On each of 119 and 179, the three choices on each side are true exterior twins of the *four-star input*; the two three-sets are disjoint. Relabelling inside these sets sends any of the nine ordered cases to the first one, including the induced `T_i` pins. This is a relabelling of possible completions, not a claim of automorphisms of a completed graph. Representatives are `(16,23)` on 119 and `(28,33)` on 179. Treating the actual matching choices as independent copies with different outside labels is invalid.

**A quantitative bound independent of the terminal SAT proof.** For *any* two full matchings on two disjoint flags there are at least **32** ordered choices of a disjoint petal pair (old lower bound 15). With `k=0,1,2` cross endpoint edges the respective bounds are 41,33,32 from the 102 frozen profiles. For each side subtract from eight the number of opposite flag sites in its wings, multiply the remaining petal lower bounds, then subtract the number of shared eligible sites. For `k=2` at least two shared sites were spent as just described. For `k=1`, if two sites are blocked on each side, either some side has both in the same petal (so at least 7×6 choices before conflicts), or the mates of the two opposite endpoints of the single cross edge are distinct shared eligible sites already spent (they are in opposite wing intersections). `book_audit.py` evaluates these bounds in original coordinates. This counts compatible pairs in the two eight-matchings, not disjoint copies of whole bouquets.

## 3. Two terminal local refutations

Suppose for contradiction that **no** disjoint marking in G has the desired packing. Every disjoint marking then has type 119 or 179 by sections 1–2. For any old anchor `s` and possible new endpoint `x`, test whether `s,x` is a nonedge of codegree one with a path disjoint from the *opposite* old marked path. If so the four endpoint stars `(s,x,c,d)` must be one of 119/179 under either ordering of each pair and exchange of the pairs. Given the three individually known stars `s,c,d`, a full candidate is described exactly by: six internal adjacency bits, eight exterior cell sizes relative to `s,c,d`, and the eight counts of x-neighbors in those cells. The alternative is **not activated** when the two triples overlap. `book_reroot.py` encodes this disjunction; `book_receipts.py` regenerates all eight projected keys with sets, separately from the producing code. Eighteen planted C22 instances with their real allowed profiles all pass; replacing that library by the empty set rejects all 18.

The small formula retains the physical unknown edges in both wing rectangles, pinned stars, forced mate edges and the independent `T_i` conditions; it uses degree/square-codegree bounds (only counting represented walks in the projection), triangle clauses for represented edges, and I10 clauses whose only open edges lie in the two rectangles. Degree and codegree lower bounds are reduced by all unrepresented possibilities. Ten-sets are generated as independent sets of the upper graph with both optional rectangles removed, then their represented optional edges form the clause. Extra variables used by the reroot test are ordinary physical edge variables. See `book_counter.py` and `book_obstructions.py`.

- On **179**, these pins and the alternative-mark restriction already contradict one another (`proof/179-general.cnf,lrat`). No packing-table clauses are needed in the terminal SAT formula beyond the hand-derived `T_i` pins.
- On **119**, also impose failure of four-plus-four on the present marking. Partition the eight shared eligible sites in all 256 ways. The two resulting disjoint pools contain four matched edges each iff the packing exists for some partition: unmatched shared sites can be assigned arbitrarily. For **each** partition at least one side must have a vertex cover C of at most three, which can be padded to size three. Enumerate the triples; require every represented edge of that pool to hit C. An option can be dropped if `W-C` contains a pinned edge, or if it would make a known independent seed T extend to a ten via its certainly nonadjacent sites in `W-C`. The cover on each partition/side can be different. The actual *edge variables* are shared over all options and both books. Now UNSAT (`proof/119-general.cnf,lrat`). The very same partition table on 18 pinned C22 controls correctly admits eight no-three-plus-three markings and rejects ten actual three-plus-three markings. Without the global alternative-mark restriction the two general projected instances both have partial counterdiagrams; those are not counterexample Ramsey graphs.
- **Only at K=2**, the 119 instance is also UNSAT without the reroot premise when every unmarked nonedge has true codegree at least two (`proof/119-two.cnf,lrat`). This does not exclude K=2 or ID 119. The ordinary all-edge unpruned cover variant capped on 119 at 22,000 conflicts. No speed claim from that variant.

All terminal UNSATs above have both CakeLRAT and `lrat-check` acceptance; CNFs were regenerated and checked a second time, not just the producing solver's output. `book_rebuild.sh` gives the exact rebuild commands (`119-general 179-general 119-two high3-179`). First CakeLRAT launches at 6 GB VM failed to initialize and were not counted as checks; the successful logs use the normal 16 GB VM ceiling.

## 4. Removing one complete root ID from the cover

Consider a graph where all nonadjacent cross endpoint pairs of **every disjoint marking** have codegree three. The only compatible roots in the confirmed 102 list are `119,121,122,175,178,179,191`. Test an occurrence of **179** in such a graph, with **no packing, extra mate or private-independence assumptions at all**. `book_select.py` uses ordinary labelled edge variables/degree/triangle/codegree clauses. It includes the three-fixed-star reroot test and a weaker but wider test for *any* additional singleton path `x-h-y` disjoint from either one old flag `a-z-b`. Its 2×2 cross-adjacency pattern, `h`'s incidence on `a,b`, `z`'s incidence on `x,y` and the four cross codegrees must occur together in a view of the seven allowed roots. The two old opposite-path sites and the witness are physical, not freely permuted auxiliary choices. The implication has antecedent both represented two-walk and codegree less than two; genuine paths meeting the old flag are not included. `book_receipts.py` independently regenerates all 27 oriented templates; all 18 planted C22 panels pass `book_select.py` with their real catalogue. No graph-size-independent Ramsey claim is made from these templates.

`proof/high3-179.cnf,lrat`: UNSAT, both checkers passed twice. Consequently any graph with an ID 179 marking has a disjoint marking *not* in those seven, and in particular neither 179 nor 119. By sections 1–2 that alternative marking carries the desired packing. If 179 never occurs, the existential bouquet theorem from section 3 applies directly. This proves the claimed 101-root **cover**, not a disjoint partition and not the nonexistence of all completions of 179. Larger exploratory allowed families are not ruled out: five samples from a 20-member family all capped at 5,500 conflicts; all four cases in `179,186,187,188` capped at 15,000. Do not extrapolate a general reroot reduction from solver speed on 179.

## 6. Whole-type exclusions and a shared-exterior pilot

**Nine of the 41 eleven-site books are impossible anywhere in general R(3)**, not just in a chosen double bouquet. For a subset S of one book B, the number of sites outside B meeting S is at least

`L(S) = sum_{v in S} (9-d_B(v)) - sum_{uv in choose(S,2)} (upper_codeg(u,v)-codeg_B(u,v))`.

This is two-term inclusion-exclusion for the outside neighborhoods; take upper codegrees 0 on edges, 1 on the designated deficient pair, 3 otherwise. There are only **29** outside sites, so `L(S)>29` is a hand-checkable obstruction. When a second disjoint book is chosen, at least `L(S)-18` distinct opposite-book sites must already meet S. `book_contacts.py` evaluates all 2,048 subsets for each type, emitting an explicit maximizing bitset. The maxima of `L-18` and their type counts are `3:1, 5:6, 7:8, 10:17, 12:6, 13:1, 14:1, 18:1`. Thus nine require >11 contacts in the other book (equivalently >29 sites outside B). `S/book-footprints.jsonl`, `S/footprints.log`; 4,608 individual planted small-case footprint inequalities replayed. **32 types, hence 528 unordered type pairs, suffice**. These are not 528 complete ten-star/22-star root cases.

Independent stronger test: assign a nonnegative count to every possible outside contact mask on the book. Require the correct total, degree residues, and all residual codegree bounds. A mask is independent, cannot witness a saturated pair and cannot already give a codegree above three with a book site. At K=2, all other nonedges internal to this book need at least two common neighbors: the second deficient pair is on the disjoint path in the other book and cannot occur internally in this argument. The nine general separations have exact integer duals; **all 32** other systems have exact rational witnesses. At K=2 there are **eleven** exact exclusions (two additional), 28 exact rational witnesses and two LP-feasible outputs without rational receipts. Upper bound **30 types / 465 type pairs** in the K=2 double-book cover, not solutions of 30 graph-extension problems. `book_single.py` checks each numerical separator or exact primal using Python integers and a second all-2^11 mask enumeration; 36 planted seven-site books pass. See `S/single-types.{jsonl,log}`. These type exclusions cut any situation satisfying their stated local lower bounds; do not apply the two conditional exclusions to an unrelated book whose other diagonals may also be deficient.

For a directly matched-price test, take two books with C8 as their leaf-to-leaf bipartite graph; start with a K2,2 on nonadjacent transversals and a witness-to-witness edge. Add cross edges in the fixed seed order in `book_outside_panel.py`, stopping at the five predetermined totals **5,8,12,16,20**. These are five particular *labelled induced 22-site boundary graphs*; they do not fix the rest of any of the four endpoint stars. All five pass all sixteen previous K=2 pentagon-interface lookups. The shared 18-site occupancy test refutes **all five, even with unrestricted extra deficiencies**. The first three already fail one of the two hand footprint tests; the last two do not. All ten integer duals (five general and five K=2) independently re-summed over the product of the two independent subset lists. `S/outside-panel.jsonl`, `S/outside.log`, `S/footprints.log`. This is **5/5 assembled frontiers**, not 5/5 four-star parent IDs, not a cut at all cross-edge counts up to 20, and not exclusion of the C8/C8 type pair. The 5/5 hit rate on this deliberately sparse, nested sample is not a population estimate. These joined constraints are the promising next pricing target, unlike the all-interface checks alone.
