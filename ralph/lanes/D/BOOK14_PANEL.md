**Eight surviving boundaries:** calibrate `2(e(Q)−e(B))=d(|Q|−|B|)` on all 18 planted 14+8 C22 boundaries (difference −18 edges there), and reject all 1,106 earlier mixed-label swaps. With 22+18 and degree 9, `e(Q)=e(B)−18`. For a contact mask M the Q-degree must be `9−|M|`. For an independent core set I, sum the number of Q-neighbors of each Q-site seeing I. This must equal `sum_M x_M (9−|M|) 1[M intersects I]` when counted by the other end. At a site with mask M this number is ≤ its Q-degree and ≤ the sum over i∈I of the remaining codegree caps (zero if i∈M). A lower bound is `|I|−|M intersect N[I]|`: each core site i not adjacent to the site needs one common neighbor, and the known core two-walks are also individually used in the singleton lower bound. More precisely the program uses `max(0, |I|−|M intersect N[I]|, 1[one required singleton has no known two-walk])`. The first lower bound follows by counting distinct Q-neighbors in the union of their neighborhoods in Q via the independent set `I + (N(q)−N[I])`, not by assuming different i require different witnesses. ## Whole half-wing and full-wing reductions

Now consider **one** deficient path. Mark its entire first wing A (8 vertices) and choose any four sites in its opposite wing B. This is a 15-site boundary (3 anchors +8+4). Every choice of four opposite sites has a matching to four A sites by Hall. Compute all 8×4 patterns with the necessary side degrees and overlaps; genbg plus an independent sorted binary-row census give **530 types / 8,234 sorted labelled row words**. Before LP: 26 violate the remaining side's pair cap (the `cap` status here is a *literal structural violation*, not a time cap), 52 have no matching of four, 48 contain a D-032 forbidden 4×4 subbook. On 404 remaining 15-site induced graphs, enumerate all independent masks hitting the boundary independent nines, excluding neighbors of the already full endpoint. Impose degree and codegree residues on the **same** 25 exterior sites. **66 exact exclusions, 338 fractional survivors; 0 numerical time caps.** `S/{half.jsonl,half-bind.log,half-replay.log}`. Compare C22: all 1,250 planted 5×2 choices bind; an actual integer exterior vector replayed for all 12 occupied smaller classes.

Complete genbg roster for the 8+8 wings at `-d1 -D3 -Z2` has 234,044 color-preserving outputs. Hall and the remaining overlap cap remove 71,550; the D-032 forbidden subbooks remove 42,229 more; the return cut removes 274 more. Among **119,991** remaining oriented complete wings, project each choice of four columns on *both orientations*, stopping on an impossible 15-site class: **75,139 more removed, 44,852 left**. This is the first test here whose denominator is the entire relevant oriented wing family rather than sampled joined boundaries. ## A final hand cut: every wing square needs three extra wing edges

On a square S (`K2,2`) of a deficient full wing F, each same-shore pair already has three common neighbors (two on S and its endpoint). Opposite-shore pairs are adjacent. **Each of the 21 exterior sites sees at most one square vertex.** The demand of the four is `32−sum_{v in S}d_F(v)`, so `sum d_F(v)>=11`. At least three of the four square sites have degree three. If exactly three do, *all* 21 exterior sites see S; if all four do, exactly 20 do. This does not assume K=2. Applying it to the intermediate 44,761/42,610 rosters removes **3,275** more complete wing matrices (3,254 rich), each with a named square. No additional non-square higher clique was needed. Final rosters **41,486 oriented wings covering all markings / 39,356 sufficient for some rich mark**. `S/{clique-all.tsv,clique-rich.tsv,clique-cuts.tsv,cliques.log}`; all witnesses rechecked in a separate set representation; positive check on all 125 planted C22 wings. These are not 39,356 globally disjoint cases.

At exactly two deficiencies the two pairs are disjoint (D-020), so each endpoint has just one deficient partner. Every vertex of its opposite wing has degree at least two, since this degree is the codegree with the endpoint. At `t=16` all are exactly two. The cycle partitions of eight of minimum part two number seven. The four containing a half-length 2 (C4) fail the hand square cut; only **C16, C10+C6, or C8+C8** remain as possible marked t=16 wings. An actual one-additional-deficiency total-debit LP gives the same four exclusions and three fractional outcomes, 0.299 CPU-s total / no caps; reproduced the true debit on 20 C22 populations. `S/cycle16-price.log`. No claim that these three extend or that the whole K=2 case is excluded.
