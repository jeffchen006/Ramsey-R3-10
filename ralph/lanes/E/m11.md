# Saturated f=32 windows: round 6

## Scope and reductions

These claims concern `(2,32,t=4,δ(W)≥2)` in the notation of
`involution.md`, with the same universal minimum-degree-five premise.
No MTF assumption is applied to the automorphism. Write `m=e(W)` and
`b_i=e(W,A_i)`. Each group `A_i` is independent of size six and every
`w∈W` has a neighbor in each. From `window73.md`, every `w` has fixed
degree eight, and for `m≥9` every attachment vertex has fixed degree
seven. Therefore `e(F)=(8·8+24·7)/2=116` and, **for each** group,

```
e(F\A_i) = e(F) − Σ_{a∈A_i} d_F(a) = 116 − 42 = 74.
```

This holds for all `m=9,10,11,12`, not just `m=11`. When `m=11`,
`Σb_i=42` and `b_i≥10`, so the two profiles are exactly
`(10,10,10,12)` and `(10,10,11,11)`, with no claim that all profiles
are realized. Every row in `W` has total surplus `4−d_W(w)` above
one neighbor per group. Its degree in a window omitting `A_i` is
`8−|N(w)∩A_i|`. Thus omitting a size-ten incidence group gives six
degree-seven W vertices (two doubles and six distinct singleton slots).
More generally at least `16−b_i` W vertices have degree seven there.

If `m=8` and the attachment degree deficit `P=2` from `window73.md`,
the four windows have sizes `73+P_i`, where `P_i` is the local deficit
of the omitted group. At least two have size 73; each requires
`b_i≥14`. Since `Σb_i=48` and all `b_i≥10`, equality forces
`(b_i)=(10,10,14,14)` and `(P_i)=(1,1,0,0)` in the same group order:
there cannot be a third size-73 window, and the other two each need
one unit of deficit. In particular both size-74 windows have six
degree-seven W vertices. If a complete 74-edge census were shown to
have at most four degree-seven vertices, it would rule out every
`m≥9` (some `b_i≤11`), and also this deficit-two branch of `m=8`.

## Reverse census: 1,624 of 1,625, not a closure

From the checked 62 order-26, 73-edge rows in `r5_saturated/hog26_e73.g6`,
addition gives 1,615 distinct classes; one critical-edge swap pass
gives nine more. All 1,624 are checked triangle-free with no independent
eight-set. The degree-seven histogram is `0:106, 1:316, 2:614, 3:428,
4:160`. GR Table 13 reports 1,625 classes, so the largest degree-seven
count of the complete census is **unknown**. In particular, any
`m=11` crown must have at least two windows isomorphic to the **same
as-yet-missing class**: both size-ten omissions have six degree-seven
W vertices and cannot be among the 1,624 known. This is a necessary
condition, not a case closure. For the `(10,10,11,11)` profile, all
four omissions have at least five degree-seven W vertices, so all
four windows must be isomorphic to that one class.

`scratch/E/r6_e11/addswap2.g6` and `catalogue_second.log` contain the
census and histogram. No improvement from additional single swaps,
same- or varying-degree star rewires, two-star replacement, or the
two-edge swap sweep. Exact smaller oracle controls: 105 order-11
rows for star/deletion, 96 two-vertex lift controls, three two-swap
controls, and 24 deletion-window controls. The 35-root pair-lift
pilot forecast 170–230 CPU s; full four-shard lifts find no new
class. The complete two-swap scan tests 669,435 edits and emits
608,379 labeled results, all already in the catalogue. Replaying the
same moves is not a completeness argument.

## Forward exact cover and proof logic

At `m=11`, `2≤d_W≤4`, and some vertex has degree two. An independent
labelled enumeration puts its neighbors at 1 and 2 and compares every
remaining nine-edge subset with the explicit permutation orbits of
the 19 keys (`m11_support.py`, 5,280 normalized labelled cores).

For a single size-six attachment with row degrees fixed, assign the
singletons to distinct slots in core-vertex order. Each remaining
row chooses all sets of slots of its specified size. A column must
avoid adjacent W pairs (triangle condition). For each independent
set `S⊆W`, if the union of its attachment neighborhoods had size
less than `|S|`, `S` plus the unused slots would have size seven.
Conversely, this Hall condition covers every independent set in
`W∪A_i`. Sorting the six columns loses nothing. `m11_support.py`
agrees with a direct labelled graph oracle on 316 smaller degree
vectors (189 local supports), and checks projected orbit selection
against Cartesian products. Profile A's size-ten support sets are
also independently generated by the earlier two-double routines.

For the mixed cover, project by omitting one largest incidence
group. Canonicalize by permutations of retained equal-size groups
and W automorphisms. For each projected window the omitted **row
degrees** are determined by the residual surplus; generate all
support columns with those row degrees. When the largest incidence
groups tie, choose the lexicographically first full tuple under
allowed permutations and W automorphisms; its projected prefix is
also minimal. For nonadjacent W vertices with no common W neighbor,
either at least two groups share a neighbor for them, or their only
shared group contains nonsingleton rows for both. Otherwise deleting
the unique shared group (or any singleton group if none is shared)
would leave a degree-seven W vertex and another vertex anticomplete
to its neighborhood, an independent eight-set. This filter uses all
four windows, not an MTF premise. The one-profile projected cover
`profile_a11.py` instead **drops** omitted group feasibility and
completely covers the necessary triples directly; independent
projection enumeration matches every retained triple orbit.

For a pinned window, known edges are W and W–A edges. The only
potential remaining edges cross distinct attachment groups with
disjoint W supports. `m11_prop.c` keeps lower and upper graphs.
Every additional forced edge comes with an independent eight-set
in the upper graph minus that edge, so any completion must retain
it. An optional edge is removed only if it closes a triangle with
already-forced edges, meets a vertex with seven forced neighbors,
or would breach the exact 74-edge target. `m11_prop.py` verifies
the witnesses and whether they terminate in an independent set,
triangle, or degree/edge bound. It is a proof mechanism, not a
decision on inputs where propagation stops. It checks 93 smaller
controls (35 positive; 45 negatives refuted).

For remaining projections, the window CNF has necessary cross-edge
triangle rows, exact 74-edge and degree-at-most-seven rows, and a
subset of the projected independent-eight-set clauses (keep up to
eight literals). Therefore a checked UNSAT refutes the window even
though SAT would not by itself be a valid window. A separate CNF
also encodes all four alpha windows and exact degree seven in each
attachment; no full-width SAT at this stage is a claim of a graph.
Ten smaller exhaustive completion counts agree with the new
encoding; `m11_fastcuts.c` agrees byte-for-byte with slow Python
on the tested width-eight pilot and every calibration cut set.

## Results and binding

- Mixed profile, **one core only**, `W = G?Beto`: 12,306 projected
  rows; 9,012 projection witnesses and 39,503 lifted alternative
  window witnesses. 1,171 lifts of 389 projected rows needed SAT;
  all 389 corresponding primary projections have checked LRATs.
  Do not count those 1,171 lifts as independent LRATs. Scope is
  precisely this core in the `m=11` branch. Evidence:
  `scratch/E/r6_e11/{census.0.log,core0.replay.log,core0.certificates.sha256}`.
- **Cert candidate, complete profile `(10,10,10,12)` over all 19
  cores.** Of 180,325 exact projected rows, 128,474 refute by graph
  witnesses and 51,845 by checked native LRAT. Six primary windows
  survive even full alpha and are not UNSAT. Their exact omitted-group
  lift has 144 supports, of which 101 refute by graph witnesses and
  43 by checked LRAT. The lift coverage agrees with independent raw
  support enumeration and all 43 proofs replay. No missing status,
  cap, unchecked final UNSAT, or candidate remains in this profile.
  Primary production consumed 2.9089 CPU-hours. Evidence and SHA-256:
  `scratch/E/r6_e11/{profile_a11.final.log,profile_a11.certificates.sha256}`.
  This overlaps the core-0 claim and adds no closure of the other
  profile beyond that core. The 97-row dispersed pilot checked and
  replayed all of its primary projections.

For the core-0 bundle, from `scratch/E/r6_e11`, run
`sha256sum -c core0.certificates.sha256` and
`python3 -B ../../../ralph/lanes/E/audit_m11.py core 0` under the

and plan; `freeze_a11.py freeze --with-rescue` writes the bindings and
replays the exact omitted-group lift. Work in a scratch copy for a
freeze replay; the command writes the exception and binding indexes.

The B-profile scale probes find 192,113 and 189,014 projected rows
surviving the support/degree-seven-domination filters at core 9 and
core 18, respectively. Their full witness scans reached their CPU
caps, so no closure follows. The calibrated two-group alpha presieve
eliminates only 11 of 6,114 feasible pair pins, and the depth-three
two-edge-clause proof tree adds only 2 proofs to 389 residual core-0
projections. Neither merits scaling in place of SAT.
